For additional information on Markets revenue, refer to page 70 of the Firms 2022 Form 10-K. d.Second-quarter 2023 net income, earnings per share and ROTCE excluding significant items are non-GAAP financial measures. Significant items collectively refer to the bargain purchase gain associated with First Republic of $2.7 billion, the net credit reserve build associated with First Republic of $1.2 billion and net investment securities losses of $900 million. Excluding these significant items resulted in a decrease of $1.1 billion (after tax) to reported net income from $14.5 billion to $13.3 billion; a decrease of $0.38 per share to reported EPS from $4.75 to $4.37; and a decrease of 2% to ROTCE from 25% to 23%.
