ANAHEIM, CALIFORNIA January 8, 2025 EACO Corporation (OTC Pink:EACO)
announced the results for its quarter ended November 30, 2024.

Net sales, net income and earnings per share were as follows for the

The Company had 426 sales employees at November 30, 2024, an increase
of 13 or 3% from the prior year quarter. The Companys sales force is divided into sales focus teams (SFTs). The Company
had 112 SFTs as of November 30, 2024, an increase of 3 from the prior year quarter. Management anticipates continued growth in
both our headcount and SFTs in fiscal year 2025. The Company believes it continues to gain market share through its local presence
business model.
