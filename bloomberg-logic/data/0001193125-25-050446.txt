Regulation FD Disclosure. 

On March 10, 2025, Funko, Inc. (the Company) clarified that its net sales guidance for the full year 2025 provided on its earnings call on March 6, 2025 should have been a range of $1.05 billion to $1.082 billion, which is consistent with the guidance provided in the Companys earnings release on the same date.
