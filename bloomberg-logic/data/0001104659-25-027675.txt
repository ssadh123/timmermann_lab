Ladies and Gentlemen:

We have acted as special counsel
to Ford Credit Auto Receivables Two LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (the "Registrant"), and Ford Credit Auto
Owner Trust 2025-A, a Delaware statutory trust, as issuer (the "Trust") in connection with the above-referenced Registration
Statement (the "Registration Statement"), and with the issuance by the Trust on the date hereof of its Asset Backed Notes,

The Trust is governed by an Amended and Restated Trust Agreement, dated as of March 1, 2025 (the "Trust Agreement"),

the Trust and The Bank of New York Mellon, as indenture trustee (the "Indenture Trustee").

(including the form of Notes included as an exhibit to the Indenture), the Underwriting Agreement, dated March 18, 2025, among the
Depositor, Ford Motor Credit Company LLC and the representatives of the several underwriters named therein, and the other transaction
