Item 8.01. 

Other Events.

On March 17, 2025, Immunocore Holdings plc (the Company) and U.S. Bank Trust Company, National Association, as trustee, entered
into the first supplemental indenture (the First Supplemental Indenture), supplementing the indenture, dated February 2, 2024 (the Indenture) governing the Company's 2.50% Convertible Senior Notes due 2030 (the Notes), to add to the Indenture
