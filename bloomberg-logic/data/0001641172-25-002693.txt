1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

April 1, 2025, American Battery Technology Company (the Company) entered into a Commercial/Investment Property Purchase
Agreement and Joint Escrow Instructions (the Purchase Agreement), pursuant to which the Company entered into an agreement
with Steven Pokrajac and Corina Pokrajac and/or their assigns to sell that certain property comprising approximately 12 acres located
