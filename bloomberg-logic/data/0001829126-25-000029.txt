Item
5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

On
December 27, 2024, Vivakor, Inc. (the Company) held its 2024 annual meeting of stockholders (the Annual
Meeting). The total shares voted at the meeting were 24,598,766, meaning greater than 50% of the shares of common stock
outstanding and entitled to vote at the Annual Meeting were present in person or by proxy thereby constituting a quorum. The results
of the matters voted on by the Companys stockholders are set forth immediately below.

Election
of the four nominees to the Companys board of directors:
