Item 3.02 

Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities

On February 14, 2025, SecureTech Innovations, Inc. (SecureTech) entered into Share Exchange Agreements with five shareholders. In these agreements, 43,100,000 shares of SecureTech's common stock were exchanged for 4,310 shares of its Series A Preferred Stock, with a par value of $0.001 per share. The common stock shares were canceled as part of SecureTechs Share Reduction Plan.

As of February 19, 2025, SecureTech had 35,309,329 shares of its common stock issued and outstanding and 17,710 shares of its Series A Preferred Stock issued and outstanding.

Item 5.02 

Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; CompensatoryArrangements of Certain Officers.

On February 14, 2025, Abdikarim Farah, SecureTechs Vice President, resigned from the company. The Board accepted Mr. Farahs resignation, which took effect immediately.

There were no disagreements between Mr. Farah and SecureTech.
