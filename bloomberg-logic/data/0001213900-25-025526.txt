Item 8.01. Other Events. 

Postponement of Extraordinary General Meeting

On March 12, 2025, SK Growth Opportunities Corporation
(the Company) filed a definitive proxy statement (the Extension Proxy Statement) for an extraordinary general
meeting (the Extension Meeting) of its shareholders originally scheduled to be held on March 27, 2025, at 4:00 p.m. Eastern
Time to approve, among other things, an amendment to the Companys amended and restated memorandum and articles of association (the
Articles) to extend the date by which the Company has to complete an initial business combination from March 31, 2025 to
June 22, 2025 (the Extension), or such earlier date as the Companys board of directors may approve in accordance
with the Articles. Defined terms used but not defined herein have the meanings set forth in the Extension Proxy Statement.

The board of directors of the Company has decided
to postpone the Extension Meeting to March 28, 2025, at 11:00 a.m., Eastern Time to allow additional time for the Company to engage with
its shareholders.

The postponed Extension Meeting will be held on
March 28, 2025, at 11:00 a.m., Eastern Time, at the offices of Wilson Sonsini Goodrich & Rosati, 650 Page Mill Road, Palo Alto, California
94304 and conducted remotely by teleconference. Shareholders can attend the postponed meeting by visiting https://www.cstproxy.com/skgrowthopportunities/ext2025.
If you do not have access to internet, you can listen only to the meeting by dialing +1 800-450-7155 (toll-free) (or +1 857-999-9155 if
you are located outside the United States and Canada (standard rates apply)) and when prompted enter the pin number 6141921#.
Please note that you will not be able to vote or ask questions at the Extension Meeting if you choose to participate telephonically.

Redemption Requests 

On March 10, 2025, the Company filed a definitive
proxy statement (the Business Combination Proxy Statement and together with the Extension Proxy Statement, the Proxy
Statements) for an extraordinary general meeting (the Business Combination Meeting and together with the Extension
Meeting, the Meetings) of its shareholders scheduled to be held on March 30, 2025, at 4:00 p.m. Eastern Time to approve,
among other things, the proposed Business Combination with Webull.

The Companys public shareholders are entitled
to submit their Public Shares for redemption in connection with both the Extension Meeting and the Business Combination Meeting. On March
3, 2025 and March 6, 2025, the redemption price per Public Share was approximately $11.69, respectively, which is expected to be the same
approximate amount two (2) business days prior to each of the Extension Meeting and Business Combination Meeting.

Shareholders who wish to ensure that their Public
Shares are redeemed in the event that either the Extension is implemented or the Business Combination is consummated must instruct the
Companys transfer agent, Continental Stock &Transfer Company (the Transfer Agent), to redeem such shares in connection
with both the Extension Meeting and the Business Combination Meeting, no later than the redemption deadline for each meeting, which is
March 26, 2025 at 5.00 p.m., Eastern Time and March 27, 2025 at 5:00 p.m., Eastern Time, respectively.

The Company cannot assure you whether it will hold
the Extension Meeting on March 28, 2025, or the Business Combination Meeting on March 30, 2025. If the Company holds the Extension Meeting
and implements the Extension, any Public Shares submitted for redemption in connection with the Extension Meeting will be redeemed; however,
Public Shares submitted for redemption solely in connection with the Business Combination Meeting will not be redeemed in connection with
the Extension, and such shareholders will remain shareholders of the Company. If the Company holds the Business Combination Meeting and
completes the Business Combination, any Public Shares submitted for redemption in connection with the Business Combination Meeting will
be redeemed; however, Public Shares submitted for redemption solely in connection with the Extension Meeting will not be redeemed in connection
with the Business Combination, and such shareholders will remain shareholders of the combined company.

If the Company does not hold the Extension Meeting
and implement the Extension, any Public Shares originally submitted for redemption in connection with the Business Combination Meeting
and also instructed to be redeemed in connection with the Extension Meeting will be automatically subject to redemption in connection
with the consummation of the Business Combination, unless you withdraw such redemption request.

Shareholders who wish to withdraw their previously
submitted redemption request may do so prior to the taking of the shareholders vote at the Business Combination Meeting and/or
the Extension Meeting, or thereafter with the Companys consent, by requesting that the transfer agent return such shares. You may
contact the Transfer Agent, at One State Street Plaza, 30th Floor, New York, New York 10004, Attn: SPAC Redemption Team, or by email at
spacredemptions@continentalstock.com.
