ITEM 2.05. COSTS ASSOCIATED WITH EXIT OR DISPOSAL ACTIVITIES.

On March 18, 2025, O-I Glass, Inc. (the
Company) finalized its plans for the closure of a plant in its European segment. This closure is part of the Companys
Fit to Win initiative to reduce redundant capacity and begin to optimize its network. Additional capacity closures and other restructuring
actions are expected in 2025.

The
Company intends to facilitate the closure in a respectful manner for the approximately 170 people impacted. Current customers of the plant
impacted by the closure will be served by other European plants in the Companys network. Subject to finalization of certain
estimates, the Company expects to record charges associated with this closure of approximately $50 million in the first quarter of 2025,
primarily for one-time employee separation benefits and other costs related to the closing (of which approximately $50 million relate
to future cash expenditures).

ITEM 8.01. OTHER EVENTS.

The Company has also approved a severance program and other restructuring
actions that is primarily expected to reduce future selling, general and administrative costs primarily in the Americas segment and in
the non-reportable Retained corporate costs and other category. The Company expects to record a charge associated with these actions of
approximately $20 million in the first quarter of 2025, all which relates to future cash expenditures.
