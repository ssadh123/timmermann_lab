Portfolio Update

As of December 31, 2024, the Fund had debt investments and equity investments in 275 portfolio companies with an aggregate of $1.7 billion (at cost) in investments and an average position size of 0.36%.2 As of December 31, 2024, the Funds investments consisted of 92.70% first-lien debt investments, 3.08% second-lien debt investments, 3.05% mezzanine debt investments, and 1.17% equity investments (at fair value). As of December 31, 2024, on a fair value basis, 94.66% of the Funds debt investments bear interest at a floating rate and 5.34% of the Funds debt investments bear a fixed rate. As of December 31, 2024, the weighted average yield on the debt and income producing investments at fair value was 9.69%.3

As of December 31, 2024, our portfolio companies had a weighted average reported EBITDA (including all private debt investments and excluding quoted assets) of $81.8 million. Including all quoted assets as of December 31, 2024, our portfolio companies had a weighted average reported EBITDA of $226.3 million. EBITDA amounts are derived from the most recently available portfolio company financial statements and are weighted based on the fair market value of each respective investment as of its most recent valuation. As of December 31, 2024, our portfolio companies had a weighted average loan-to-value of 40.20%.4
