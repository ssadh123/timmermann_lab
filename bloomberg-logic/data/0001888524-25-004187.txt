Item 6.02 Change of Servicer or Trustee.

The 1745 Broadway Mortgage Loan, which
constituted approximately 9.96% of the asset pool of the issuing entity as of
its cut-off date, is an asset of the issuing entity and is part of a loan
combination that is being serviced and administered under the trust and
servicing agreement, dated as of July 2, 2018, relating to the Wells Fargo
Commercial Mortgage Trust 2018-1745 filed as Exhibit 4.2 to the registrants
Current Report on Form 8-K filed on August 2, 2018 (the WFCM 2018-1745 TSA). 
Pursuant to Section 7.1(d) of the WFCM 2018-1745 TSA, Argentic Services Company LP, a Delaware limited
partnership, was removed as special
servicer of the 1745 Broadway Mortgage Loan and CWCapital Asset Management LLC
(CWCAM), a Delaware limited liability company, was appointed as the
successor special servicer of the 1745 Broadway Mortgage Loan under the WFCM
2018-1745 TSA.

This Current Report on Form 8-K is being filed to
record that, effective as of March 6, 2025, the 1745 Broadway Mortgage Loan
will be specially serviced, if necessary, pursuant to the WFCM 2018-1745 TSA,
by CWCAM. CWCAM maintains a servicing office at 900 19th Street NW, 8th
Floor, Washington, D.C. 20006.
