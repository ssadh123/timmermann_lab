Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure.

On April 2, 2025, President Donald J. Trump announced a plan that would implement a broad range of tariffs on goods imported into the United States. Axogen, Inc. (the "Company") anticipates minimal impact from these tariffs, as well as from any retaliatory actions taken by other countries, on its current financials and underlying business model. The vast majority of the Company's sales are domestic, as is the manufacturing of its products. The costs associated with imported materials needed for its operations is minimal.
