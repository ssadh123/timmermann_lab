Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

(a) On March 14, 2025, Blue Bird Corporation (the Company) held its annual meeting of stockholders. The Company solicited proxies for the meeting pursuant to Regulation 14A under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the Exchange Act). As of the record date of January 15, 2025, there were 32,111,078 shares of common stock outstanding and entitled to vote at the annual meeting. At the annual meeting, a total of 27,661,497 shares, or 86.1% of the outstanding shares of common stock, were voted by proxy or in person.

(b) The following matters were voted upon by stockholders at the annual meeting:
