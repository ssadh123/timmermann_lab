Expanding our competitive advantage with sustainable aviation fuel
(SAF) DGD Port Arthur SAF Project Large-scale SAF project at the DGD Port Arthur plant was completed in the fourth quarter of 2024 The plant has the capability to upgrade up to 50% of its current renewable diesel production capacity
to SAF, or ~235 million gallons per year The project cost was $315 million, with half of that attributable to Valero Project includes a heater, a fractionation unit to separate the SAF and renewable diesel product streams and
additional product tankage Project is expected to exceed our minimum return threshold of an after-tax IRR of 25% Under the Inflation Reduction Act (IRA), SAF receives a higher Clean Fuel Production Credit value than renewable diesel,
resulting in higher margin for SAF production SAF supports airlines compliance with global mandates and reduces their offset obligations Valero is independently evaluating an Ethanol-to-Jet process that would convert ethanol
