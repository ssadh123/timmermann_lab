AB Announces January 31, 2025 Assets Under Management

Nashville, TN, February 12, 2025 - AllianceBernstein L.P. (AB) and AllianceBernstein Holding L.P. (AB Holding) (NYSE: AB) today announced that preliminary assets under management increased to $809 billion during January 2025 from $792 billion at the end of December. The 2% increase in month-end AUM was primarily driven by market appreciation, coupled with slightly positive firmwide net flows. By channel, net inflows into Retail and Institutions were partially offset by net outflows in Private Wealth.
