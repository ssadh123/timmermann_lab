Item 8.01 Other Events.

For the year ended December 31, 2024, InPoint Commercial Real Estate Income, Inc. (the "Company") paid common stock cash distributions of approximately $12.6 million and preferred stock cash distributions of approximately $6.0 million. For income tax purposes for the common stock distributions, 0.7% were treated as ordinary dividends and 99.3% were treated as nondividend distributions (which are treated for income tax purposes as a return of capital to the extent of a stockholder's basis in its shares and thereafter as capital gain). For income tax purposes for the preferred stock distributions, all of the distributions were treated as ordinary dividends.
