Item
5.07. Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

On
March 10, 2025, Hepion Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (the Company) held its 2024 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the Meeting).

As
of January 17, 2025, the record date for the Meeting, there were 6,958,530 shares of our common
stock outstanding (including 85,581 shares of common stock issuable upon conversion of the Series A Preferred Stock).

the Meeting, the stockholders voted on the following four proposals and cast their votes as follows:

stockholders and until their respective successors have been duly elected and qualified consisting of John P. Brancaccio, Timothy Block,
