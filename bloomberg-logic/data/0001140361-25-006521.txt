Item 5.07 

Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

The Annual Meeting of Stockholders of Hologic, Inc. (the Company) was held on February 26, 2025. Of the 225,723,107 shares outstanding and entitled to vote,
210,785,549 shares were represented at the meeting, constituting a quorum of 93.38%.

All eight director nominees were elected to the Board for a one-year term.

In addition to electing directors, the stockholders:

provided advisory approval of the Companys executive compensation (say-on-pay);

ratified the appointment of Ernst & Young LLP as the Companys independent registered public accounting firm for the fiscal year ending September 27, 2025; and

provided advisory approval of a stockholder proposal to replace the supermajority voting provisions in the Companys charter and bylaws with a simple majority voting standard.
