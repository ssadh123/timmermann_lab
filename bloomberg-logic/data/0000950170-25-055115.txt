Item 5.08 Shareholder Director Nominations

On April 17, 2025, the Board of Directors of DynaResource, Inc. (the Company) established June 23, 2025 as the date of the Companys 2025 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the 2025 Annual Meeting) and April 28, 2025 as the record date for determining stockholders entitled to notice of and to vote at the 2025 Annual Meeting. The Company will provide additional meeting details in its proxy statement related to the 2025 Annual Meeting.

Because the date of the 2025 Annual Meeting is more than thirty days in advance of the anniversary date of the 2024 Annual Meeting of Stockholders, which was held on January 6, 2025, the Company is providing revised deadlines for any stockholder proposals, including director nominations, intended to be considered at the 2025 Annual Meeting. Pursuant to the advance notice provisions of the Companys bylaws, a stockholder interested in bringing business before the 2025 Annual Meeting or nominating a director candidate for election at the 2025 Annual Meeting must provide written notice to the Companys Secretary at 222 West Las Colinas Blvd., Suite 1910 North Tower, Irving, Texas 75039, by no later than 5:00 p.m., Central time, on April 28, 2025. Any stockholder proposals intended to be considered for inclusion in the Companys proxy materials for the 2025 Annual Meeting must also comply with the requirements and receipt deadline set forth above. In addition, all stockholder proposals must comply with all applicable provisions of the Companys bylaws, and all applicable rules and regulations promulgated by the Securities and Exchange Commission under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended.
