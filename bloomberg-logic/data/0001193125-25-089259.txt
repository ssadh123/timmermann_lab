Item 7.01. 

Regulation FD Disclosure. 

Portfolio and Business Commentary 

(All figures as of March 31, 2025, unless otherwise noted) 

For the quarter ended March 31, 2025, the Companys net asset value (NAV) per share was $22.88, compared to $23.39 as of December 31, 2024. The quarter-over-quarter change in NAV per share was driven primarily by unrealized losses from certain of the Companys investments in the portfolio. Dividends for the quarter ended March 31, 2025 was $0.55 per share, representing an annualized distribution yield of 9.62% based on first quarter NAV per share.1 The Companys year-to-date return through March 31, 2025 was 0.17%. 

As of March 31, 2025, the Companys portfolio was $523.5 million based on fair market value across 60 portfolio companies and 18 industries. The Companys unfunded commitments was $23.8 million at March 31, 2025. Given our focus on middle market borrowers and our conservative structuring approach, our portfolio remained defensively positioned with 97% first lien debt investments and 99% floating rate debt investments based on fair market value. As of March 31, 2025, there were two investments placed on non-accrual status, unchanged from prior quarter end. 

We continue to operate around the low end of our target leverage ratio of 0.75x to 1.25x net debt to equity and remain focused on optimizing our funding sources and liquidity. As of March 31, 2025, the Companys net leverage ratio was 1.07x.2 As of March 31, 2025, the Company had remaining facility sizes totaling $101.0 million.3

Since inception and as of March 31, 2025, the Company has closed on 81 portfolio investments totaling over $900 million of commitments at the time of original closing. At the time of original closing for these 80 borrowers, the weighted average senior leverage was approximately 4.5 times, the loan-to-value ratio was approximately 40%, and the weighted average spread over reference rate to the London Interbank Offered Rate (LIBOR), Secured Overnight Financing Rate (SOFR), and Canadian Dollar Offered Rate (CDOR) was 619 bps. As of March 31, 2025, there were 60 active borrowers, 33 of which are considered platform investments in the portfolio, with total outstanding commitments, including unfunded loan commitments, of approximately $571.0 million.
