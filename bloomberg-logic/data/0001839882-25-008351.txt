Press Release For Immediate Release

February 11, 2025

Muncy Columbia Financial Corporation Declares Dividend

Bloomsburg, PA Muncy Columbia Financial Corporation (Corporation)
(OTCQX: CCFN), parent company of Journey Bank (Bank), announced that on February 11, 2025, the Corporations Board
of Directors declared a regular quarterly cash dividend.

Dividend Declared 

On February 11, 2025, the Corporations Board of Directors declared
a regular quarterly cash dividend of $0.45 per share for the first quarter of 2025. The dividend is payable on March 13, 2025, to shareholders
of record as of February 25, 2025. The first quarter 2025 dividend of $0.45 per share represents an increase of $0.01 per share compared
to the fourth quarter 2024 dividend of $0.44 per share.

About Muncy Columbia Financial Corporation 

Muncy Columbia Financial Corporation (MCFC)
is a registered financial holding company headquartered in Bloomsburg, Pennsylvania. MCFC has one subsidiary bank, Journey Bank, serving
individuals, families, nonprofits and business clients throughout Clinton, Columbia, Lycoming, Montour, Northumberland and Sullivan Counties
through 22 banking offices.
