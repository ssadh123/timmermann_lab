Item 8.01 Other Events.

On March 1, 2025, Lear Corporation (the Company) promoted Amanda J. Pontes to serve as the Companys General Counsel. Ms. Pontes previously served as the Companys Vice President, Global Legal and Corporate Secretary, and she will continue in the Corporate Secretary role. Ms. Pontes will continue to report to Harry A.
