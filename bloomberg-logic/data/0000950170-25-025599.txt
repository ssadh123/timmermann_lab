Item 1.01. Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

Pursuant to the first Amendment to the Master Transaction Agreement, dated April 11, 2023 (the First Amendment), for each calendar month beginning with January 31, 2024 and ending when the VRM Full Return (as defined in the Master Transaction Agreement dated March 9, 2022 (as amended, the MTA)) is paid in full, MSP Recovery, Inc. (the Company) is required to either: (i) pay in cash or (ii) issue a warrant to purchase a number of shares of Class A Common Stock of the Company, or some combination thereof, to Virage Recovery Master LP (VRM) equal to the quotient of 1% of the calendar month-end balance of the Unpaid Base Amount (as defined in the MTA) (the Required Monthly Issuance).

To date, the Company has issued 10 warrants to VRM pursuant to the First Amendment, entitling Virage to purchase 9,751,339 shares of Class A Common Stock at a purchase price of $0.0025 per share for a period of two years from issuance, exercisable on a cashless basis only.

In connection with negotiations to restructure the Companys obligations under the MTA, on February 18, 2025, the Company entered into a term sheet agreement with Virage to amend the MTA, whereby Virage and the Company agreed, subject to certain conditions, to enter into definitive documentation at a later date to: (i) exercise the VRM Warrants to purchase that number of shares that would result in Virage owning 33 1/3% of the then issued and outstanding Class A Common Stock (the Warrant Exercise), (ii) surrender to the Company any remaining unexercised VRM Warrants, or portions thereof, for termination, (iii) contemporaneous with the Warrant Exercise, terminate its agreement to hold no more than 9.99% of the outstanding Common Stock of the Company, (iv) subject to certain conditions, grant proxy voting rights to the MRCS Principals over an amount of shares of Class A Common Stock issuable to Virage from the Warrant Exercise such that the MRCS Principals will have voting control over 51% of the total outstanding Parent Class A Common Stock, and (v) terminate any obligation of the Company to satisfy the Required Monthly Issuance.

Capitalized but undefined terms have the same meaning as set forth in the MTA.
