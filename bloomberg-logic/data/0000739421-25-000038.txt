Item 8.01.Other Events.

As previously disclosed, Alletta M. Schadler retired from the Board of Directors of the Company
(the Board) and from the Board of Directors of First Citizens Community Bank (the Bank Board) immediately prior to the Annual Meeting. Following Ms. Schadlers retirement, the size of the Board was reduced from thirteen (13) to twelve (12)
directors, and the size of the Bank Board was reduced from fourteen (14) to thirteen (13) directors.

On April 16, 2025, the Company issued a press release related to the Annual Meeting. A copy of the
press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated by reference herein.

CITIZENS FINANCIAL SERVICES, INC. HOLDS ANNUAL MEETING

Mansfield, Pennsylvania; April 17, 2025. Citizens Financial Services, Inc. held its 2025 annual meeting of shareholders (the Annual Meeting) on April 15,
2025 at First Citizens Community Bank, 11499 Route 6, Wellsboro, Pennsylvania.

At the Annual Meeting, John P. Painter II was duly elected to serve as a Class 1 director for a two-year term, and until his successor is elected and
qualified. Class 1 directors will serve until the April 2027 Annual Meeting.

At the Annual Meeting, the following four Class 2 directors were duly elected to serve for three-year terms, and until their successors are elected and
qualified: Thomas E. Freeman, Christopher W. Kunes, Terry B. Osborne, and David Z. Richards, Jr. The four Class 2 directors will serve until the April 2028 Annual Meeting.

The following corporate directors retained their positions but were not standing for election this year: Randall E. Black, Rinaldo A. DePaola, Janie M.
Hilfiger, Mickey L. Jones, Robert W. Chappell, Roger C. Graham, Jr., and R. Joseph Landy.

Additionally, shareholders voted (1) to ratify the appointment of S.R. Snodgrass, A.C., Certified Public Accountants, as the independent auditor for the
Company for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2025, and (2) to approve the compensation of the Companys named executive officers as disclosed in the proxy statement.
