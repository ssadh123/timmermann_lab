Item 8.01. 

Other Events. 

On March 28, 2025, HA Sustainable Infrastructure Capital, Inc. (the Company), entered into an amendment (the Third Amendment) to the credit agreement (the Credit Agreement), dated as of April 12, 2024 and as previously amended on September 10, 2024 (the First Amendment) and October 31, 2024 (the Second Amendment), for the Companys $1.350 billion, 4-year unsecured CarbonCount-based revolving credit facility with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as administrative agent and Bank of Montreal and M&T Bank as lenders. As described in the Third Amendment, the Company partially utilized the accordion feature of the Credit Agreement to increase available revolving commitments by $200 million (from $1.350 billion to $1.550 billion). 

Copies of the Credit Agreement, the First Amendment, the Second Amendment and the Third Amendment are filed as Exhibits 1.1, 1.2, 1.3 and 1.4 to this Current Report on Form 8-K, and the descriptions of the terms of the Credit Agreement, the First Amendment, the Second Amendment and the Third Amendment in this Item 1.01 are qualified in their entirety by reference to such Exhibits, which are incorporated herein by reference.
