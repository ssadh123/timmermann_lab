Item 1.02 

Termination of Material Definitive Agreement.

Effective January 10, 2025, Vincerx Pharma, Inc. (the Company) terminated the Sales Agreement with Leerink Partners LLC dated March 29, 2024 (the Sales Agreement), pursuant to which the Company could offer and sell shares of the Companys common stock having an aggregate offering price of up to $50,000,000 in transactions deemed to be at-the-market offerings as defined in Rule 415(a)(4) of the Securities Act of 1933 (the ATM Offering), and the ATM Offering for which the Company filed a prospectus supplement and accompanying prospectus dated March 29, 2024 (the ATM Prospectus). Prior to the termination of the Sales Agreement and the ATM Offering, the Company sold an aggregate of $2,474,596.53 of its common stock under the Sales Agreement pursuant to the ATM Prospectus.
