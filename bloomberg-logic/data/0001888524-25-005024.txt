Item 8.01 Other Events.

The75 Broad Street Mortgage Loan, which constituted
approximately 3.4% of the asset pool of the issuing entity as of its cut-off
date, is an asset of the issuing entity and is part of a loan combination that
is being serviced and administered under the trust and servicing agreement, dated as of May 1, 2017, relating to the Natixis
Commercial Mortgage Securities Trust 2017-75B filed as Exhibit 4.3 to the
registrants Current Report on Form 8-K filed on June 12, 2017 (the NCMS
2017-75B TSA). Pursuant to Section 7.1(d) of the NCMS 2017-75B TSA, KeyBank
National Association, a national banking association, was removed as special
servicer of the 75 Broad Street Mortgage Loan and Torchlight Loan Services, LLC
(Torchlight), a Delaware limited liability company, was appointed as the
successor special servicer of the75 Broad
Street Mortgage Loan under the NCMS 2017-75B TSA.

In the interest of transaction
management, this Current Report on Form 8-K is being filed to record that,
effective as of March 21, 2025, the 75 Broad Street Mortgage Loan will be
specially serviced, if necessary, pursuant to the NCMS 2017-75B TSA, by Torchlight. Torchlight maintains its
principal special servicing office at 90 Park Avenue, 20th Floor, New York, New
York 10016 and its telephone number is 212-883-2800.
