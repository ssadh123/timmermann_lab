Item 6.04 Failure to Make a Required Distribution.

The
Certificate Administrator made distributions to the Certificateholders on the
March 12, 2025 Distribution Date. The Certificate Administrator was
subsequently notified by the Master Servicer on March 17, 2025 of receipt of
funds from the late payoff of the Kaiser Center Mortgage Loan (Loan Number 6 on
Annex A-1 of the prospectus supplement of the registrant relating to the
issuing entity filed on July 31, 2015 pursuant to Rule 424(b)(5)). This
resulted in an additional principal payment to the Class A-3 Certificateholders
in an amount equal to $38,787,733.65 and an additional principal payment to the
Class A-4 Certificateholders in an amount equal to $8,950,190.39. The
additional payments were made to the Class A-3 and Class A-4 Certificateholders
on March 19, 2025.
