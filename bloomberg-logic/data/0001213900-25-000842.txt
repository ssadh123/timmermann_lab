Item 5.07. Submission
of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

On December 24, 2024,
Northern Star Investment Corp. IV (the Company) held a Special Meeting of Stockholders (the Meeting). An aggregate
of 11,510,009 shares of the Companys common stock, which represents a quorum of the outstanding common stock entitled to vote as
of the record date of December 5, 2024, were represented in person or by proxy at the Meeting.

The Companys stockholders
voted on the following proposal at the Meeting, which was approved:

(1) Proposal No. 1
The Dissolution Proposal a proposal to approve the liquidation and dissolution of the Company and the related
Plan of Liquidation and Dissolution. The following is a tabulation of the votes with respect to this proposal, which was approved by the
Companys stockholders:

For Against Abstain Broker Non-Votes

11,510,009 0 0 0

Following the meeting,
the Company executed a Certificate of Dissolution to effectuate the dissolution and liquidation of the Company.
