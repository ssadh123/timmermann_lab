Item 1.01. Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement. 

On April 23, 2025, AGCO Corporation (AGCO or the Company) and Tractors and Farm Equipment Limited (TAFE) entered into a second amendment (the Amendment) to the Amended and Restated Letter Agreement dated as of April 24, 2019, between AGCO and TAFE, as amended by Amendment No. 1, dated as of April 24, 2024(the Existing Agreement, and together with the Amendment, the Letter Agreement). The Amendment extends the expiration date of the Letter Agreement from April 24, 2025 to June 30, 2025. The Company is currently in advanced discussions with TAFE regarding a potential settlement to resolve outstanding issues between the parties.

The foregoing description of the Amendment does not purport to be complete and is subject to, and qualified in its entirety by reference to, the Amendment, a copy of which is attached hereto as Exhibit 10.1 and is incorporated herein by reference.
