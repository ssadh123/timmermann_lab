Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure

This report is filed quarterly to disclose assets
under management (AUM) by asset manager, prior to the availability of Principal Financial Group, Inc.s (the
Company) quarterly earnings release. The amounts presented herein will be consistent with the format of AUM by asset manager
and presented again in such format within the Companys financial supplement for the quarter ended March 31, 2025, when that
document is posted to the Companys investor relations web site on or about April 24, 2025.

As of March 31, 2025, the assets under management
for Principal Financial Group were $717.9 billion, of which $555.8 billion is managed by Principal Asset Management - Investment Management
and $133.5 billion is managed by Principal Asset Management - International Pension.

Principal Financial Group delivers products and
services through our network of focused investment teams. During times of market dislocations and a wider dispersion of returns, it is
important to note our allocation to small, mid-cap, international, and REIT products in equity AUM and high yield and preferred securities
allocation in fixed income AUM. Principals non-U.S. reported AUM is translated to U.S. dollar equivalents using end of reporting
period spot foreign exchange rates. For the quarter ended March 31, 2025, a combination of foreign currency translation, market performance,
and other items not reported as part of the Companys net cash flow had an approximate 1.4% positive impact as a percentage of beginning
period AUM.
