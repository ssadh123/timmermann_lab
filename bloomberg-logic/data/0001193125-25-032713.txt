communities and 26 states MRP is a publicly traded REIT that was created by way of a spin-off from Lennar in February 2025 Mutually beneficial relationship with the nations leading, investment-grade homebuilder (Baa2 / BBB); anchor
tenant with ongoing demand for finished homesite inventory MRPs simple business model generates consistent and steady income from monthly option fee payments on capital deployed, providing highly predictable revenues and cash flows
