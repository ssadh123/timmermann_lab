NORWOOD FINANCIAL CORP 

ANNOUNCES CASH DIVIDEND 

HONESDALE, PENNSYLVANIA MARCH 31, 2025 

James O. Donnelly, President and Chief Executive Officer of Norwood Financial Corp (NASDAQ Global MarketNWFL) and its subsidiary Wayne
Bank, announced that the Board of Directors has declared a $0.31 per share cash dividend, which is payable on May 1, 2025, to stockholders of record as of April 15, 2025. The $0.31 per share is a $0.01 increase over the per share dividend
declared in the first quarter of 2024. 

Mr. Donnelly commented, The Board is extremely pleased to provide our shareholders with
this quarterly dividend. It reflects the Companys financial strength and strong capital position which has contributed to our solid performance. 

Norwood Financial Corp, through its subsidiary, Wayne Bank operates fifteen offices in Northeastern Pennsylvania and fourteen offices in
Delaware, Sullivan, Ontario, Otsego and Yates Counties, New York. As of December 31, 2024, Norwood had total assets of $2.317 billion, loans outstanding of $1.693 billion, total deposits of $1.859 billion and total capital of
$214 million. The Companys stock is traded on the Nasdaq Global Market under the symbol NWFL.
