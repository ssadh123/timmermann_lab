Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

On March 11, 2025, Interactive Strength Inc. (the "Company") held a special meeting of stockholders (the Special Meeting) at 10:00 a.m. Central Time, in person at 1005 Congress Avenue, Suite 925, Austin, TX 78701, to vote on the proposals identified in the Companys definitive proxy statement filed with U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on February 14, 2025. As of January 17, 2025, the record date of the Meeting, there were a total of 1,402,126 shares of the Companys common stock, par value $0.0001 per share (the Common Stock) outstanding and entitled to vote at the Meeting. At the Special Meeting, 622,861 shares of Common Stock were represented in person or by proxy, constituting a quorum.

At the Special Meeting, the Companys stockholders were asked to consider and vote upon the following proposals:

Proposal One: Approve the Issuance of Common Stock (the "Purchase Agreement")

To approve the issuance of 20% or more of the Companys common stock issuable upon the conversion of certain notes and exercise of certain warrants issued in connection with our financing pursuant to the Purchase Agreement, in accordance with Nasdaq Listing Rule 5635(d).

The issuance of Common Stock pursuant to the Purchase Agreement was approved by the Companys stockholders. The voting results were as follows:
