Item 6.02 Change of Servicer or Trustee.

The
Fresno Fashion Fair Mall Mortgage Loan, which constituted approximately 5.1% of
the asset pool of the issuing entity as of its cut-off date, is an asset of the
issuing entity and is part of a loan combination that is being serviced and
administered under the pooling and servicing agreement, dated as of November 1,
2016, relating to the JPMDB Commercial Mortgage Securities Trust 2016-C4 filed
as Exhibit 4.3 to the registrants Current Report on Form 8-K filed on November
23, 2016 (the JPMDB 2016-C4 PSA). Pursuant to Section 7.01(d) of the
JPMDB 2016-C4 PSA, Midland Loan Services, a Division of PNC Bank, National
Association, a national banking association, was removed as special servicer of
the Fresno Fashion Fair Mall Mortgage Loan and Rialto Capital Advisors, LLC
(RCA), a Delaware limited liability company, was appointed as the successor
special servicer of the Fresno Fashion Fair Mall Mortgage Loan under the JPMDB
2016-C4 PSA.

This
Current Report on Form 8-K is being filed to record that, effective as of
January 29, 2025, the Fresno Fashion Fair Mall Mortgage Loan will be
specially serviced, if necessary, pursuant to the JPMDB 2016-C4 PSA, by
RCA. RCA maintains its principal servicing office at 200 S. Biscayne
Blvd., Suite 3550, Miami, Florida 33131.
