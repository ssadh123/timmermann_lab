Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders

Otter Tail Corporation (the Company) held its Annual Shareholder Meeting on April 14, 2025, in a virtual format. A total of 41,870,355 shares of the Companys common stock were entitled to vote as of the close of business on February 13, 2025, the record date for the Annual Meeting, of which 34,012,712 were voted in person or by proxy at the Annual Meeting, with virtual attendance constituting in person attendance at the Annual Meeting. The matters voted upon and approved by the Companys shareholders were:

1.the election of three members to the Board of Directors;

2.the approval, in a non-binding advisory vote, of the compensation provided to the Named Executive Officers as described in the Proxy Statement; and

3.the ratification of the appointment of Deloitte & Touche LLP as the Companys independent registered public accounting firm for the year 2025.
