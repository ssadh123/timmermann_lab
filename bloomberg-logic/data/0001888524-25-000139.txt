Item 6.04 Failure to Make a Required Distribution.

The
Certificate Administrator made distributions to the Certificateholders on the
December 13, 2024 Distribution Date. The Certificate Administrator was
subsequently notified by the Master Servicer on January 16, 2025 of receipt of
a curtailment payment on the ICON Upper East Side Portfolio Mortgage Loan (Loan
Number 13 on Annex A-1 of the prospectus of the registrant relating to the
issuing entity filed on June 27, 2019 pursuant to Rule 424(b)(2)). This
resulted in additional principal payments to the Class A-2 Certificateholders
in an amount equal to $1,373,284.00. The additional principal payments
were made to the Class A-2 Certificateholders on January 17, 2025.
