Item 3.02 Unregistered Sale of Equity Securities.

During January 2025, Ares Strategic Income Fund (the Fund) sold Class I common shares of beneficial interest. The number of shares to be issued was finalized on January 21, 2025. The purchase price per Class I common share equaled the Funds net asset value (NAV) per Class I common share as of December 31, 2024. The offer and sale of the Class I common shares was exempt from the registration provisions of the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, pursuant to Section 4(a)(2) thereof and/or Regulation S promulgated thereunder.
