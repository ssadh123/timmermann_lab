Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers 

On February 4, 2025, Robin L. Washington notified the Board of Directors (the Board) of Vertiv Holdings Co (the Company) that she has decided to resign from the Board, effective as of March 31, 2025, in light of her recently announced appointment to serve as President and Chief Operating and Financial Officer of Salesforce, Inc. Ms. Washingtons decision to resign from the Board was not the result of any disagreement relating to the Companys operations, policies or practices. The Company thanks Ms. Washington for her commitment and service.
