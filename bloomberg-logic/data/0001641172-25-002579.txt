5.07. Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

On
March 31, 2025, Roadzen Inc. (the Company) held an Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (the Annual Meeting).
On March 19, 2025, the record date for the Annual Meeting, there were 74,259,298 ordinary shares of the Company, par value $0.0001 per
share (Ordinary Shares) entitled to be voted at the Annual Meeting. At the Annual Meeting, 42,569,517 of the Ordinary Shares,
or approximately 57.33% of the shares entitled to vote at the Annual Meeting, were represented in person or by proxy.
