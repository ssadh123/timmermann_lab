RAYMOND JAMES FINANCIAL REPORTS JANUARY 2025 OPERATING DATA

ST. PETERSBURG, Fla. - Raymond James Financial, Inc. (NYSE: RJF) today reported selected operating data for January 2025, in an effort to provide timely information to investors about monthly developments in certain key performance metrics. Due to the limited nature of this data, a consistent correlation to earnings should not be assumed.

Client assets under administration grew 15% year-over-year and 2% over the preceding month primarily due to higher equity markets and modest net inflows in January, said Chair and CEO Paul Reilly. Clients domestic cash sweep and Enhanced Savings Program (ESP) balances of $57.3 billion were flat compared to the prior-year period and down 4% compared to the preceding month, primarily the result of quarterly fee billings and client reinvestment activity. Our investment banking pipeline continues to be healthy, although the timing of closings is difficult to predict. Bank loans grew 8% year-over-year and 1% over the preceding month, reflecting continued growth in securities-based loans.
