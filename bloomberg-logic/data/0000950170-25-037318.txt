Information regarding the collateral auction contained in Item 8.01 is incorporated by reference herein.

Item 8.01 Other Events

Nuburu, Inc. (the Company) previously announced that its secured lenders established March 5, 2025 as the auction date for the lenders sale of collateral securing the Companys repayment obligations to such lenders. The auction was concluded on March 5, 2025, as anticipated, and resulted in the transfer of collateral to an affiliate of the senior secured lenders in exchange for a full discharge and extinguishment of the Companys junior and senior secured notes.

100% of the Companys outstanding long-term indebtedness has now been eliminated through a combination of the Companys conversion of outstanding indebtedness over the course of the last year and the discharge and extinguishment of debt resulting from the lenders collateral sale.

The Company believes that the intellectual property and key personnel and relationships it retains will be sufficient to allow it to continue to develop and operate within the laser technology field, with focused attention on applications in the defense, semiconductor and industrial manufacturing sectors.

As previously announced, the Company also entered into a commitment letter with Trumar Capital LLC to acquire: (i) a license and development agreement that would allow the Company to expand its existing business within the defense sector; and (ii) a controlling interest in a defense-tech company that specializes in the design, production, and outfitting of a diverse range of vehicles, including industrial and military applications, as well as electronic devices for defense and security, advanced telecommunications, and tracking systems.Effective as of March 11, 2025, the Company has completed the acquisition of the initial 20% interest with respect to this transaction.

The elimination of all long-term indebtedness, the recently announced financing with Indigo Capital, and the expansion of business through recently announced acquisitions are part of the Companys previously announced Transformation Plan.
