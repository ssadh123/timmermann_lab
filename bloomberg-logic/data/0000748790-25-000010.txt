Item 2.03. Creation of a Direct Financial Obligation or an Obligation under an Off-Balance Sheet Arrangement of a Registrant.

On April 3, 2025, certain subsidiaries of Global Clean Energy Holdings, Inc. (we, us, our and the Company) entered into Amendment No. 22 to the Companys senior secured term loan credit agreement (the Senior Credit Agreement), by and among BKRF OCB, LLC (the Borrower), BKRF OCP, LLC, Bakersfield Renewable Fuels, LLC, Orion Energy Partners TP Agent, LLC, in its capacity as the administrative agent (the Administrative Agent), and the lenders party thereto (Amendment No. 22). Amendment No. 22 provides for, among other things, an upsizing of the Tranche D commitments under the Senior Credit Agreement of up to $380,650,000 (the Upsize), of which (i) Tranche D lenders provided commitments for $370,650,000 (that is, commitments for $10,000,000 of the Upsize were provided pursuant to Amendment No. 22) and (ii) any unfunded portion will automatically terminate on April 15, 2025 (or such later date as the Administrative Agent may consent to). In consideration for the Upsize, Amendment No. 22 provides that (i) an aggregate of $20,000,000 of Tranche A, Tranche B, or Tranche C loans, as applicable, outstanding under the Senior Credit Agreement will be recharacterized as Tranche C+ loans and (ii) up to an additional aggregate of $20,000,000 of Tranche A, Tranche B or Tranche C loans, as applicable, may be recharacterized as Tranche C+ loans to the extent the uncommitted portion of the Upsize is ultimately funded. Such Tranche C+ loans provide for a minimum return of 1.35x. 

The foregoing description of Amendment No. 22 is qualified in its entirety by reference to such agreement, a copy of which is filed herewith as Exhibit 10.1 and incorporated herein by reference.

WHEREAS, the Borrower, Holdings, the Administrative Agent, Orion Energy Partners TP Agent, LLC, in its capacity as the collateral agent, and each Tranche A Lender, Tranche B Lender, Tranche C Lender, Tranche C+ Lender and Tranche D Lender from time to time party thereto have entered into that certain Credit Agreement, dated as of May 4, 2020 (as amended, amended and restated, modified and supplemented on or prior to the date hereof, the Credit Agreement, and the Credit Agreement as expressly amended by this Agreement, the Amended Credit Agreement);

WHEREAS, the Borrower and the Lenders entered into the Credit Agreement based on certain estimated costs to install, develop and construct the Project; 

WHEREAS, pursuant to Amendment No. 21 to Credit Agreement, dated as of February 27, 2025, by and among the Borrower, Holdings, the Project Company, the Administrative Agent and the Lenders party thereto, the parties upsized the Tranche D Commitments to an aggregate principal amount of up to $360,650,000 (the Tranche D Facility);

WHEREAS, the Credit Agreement needs to be revised to more accurately reflect the updated scope and cost estimates of the Project; 

WHEREAS, in order to fund the installation, development, construction and operation of the Project, the parties hereto have determined that the Tranche D Facility may need to be upsized to an aggregate principal amount of up to $380,650,000; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to this Agreement, the Borrower has requested, and the parties hereto have agreed, subject to the satisfaction of the conditions precedent set forth in this Agreement, to amend the Credit Agreement effective as of the Twenty-Second Amendment Effective Date as set forth herein.

(ii)each of the parties hereto acknowledges and agrees that the Tranche D Commitments may be further upsized by Tranche D Lenders to an aggregate principal amount of up to $380,650,000 (the Additional Tranche D Upsizing Amounts) so long as (A) the Administrative Agent has reasonably determined that such increase is required by the Borrower and (B) no Lender shall be forced to participate in such increase without its written consent;

(such aggregate conversion amount, the Tranche C+ Conversion Amount).
