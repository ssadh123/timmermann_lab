Item 5.07Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders

On February 19, 2025, Daily Journal Corporation (the Company) held its 2025 Annual Meeting of Shareholders. A total of 1,329,312 shares were represented in person or by valid proxy, and the final results for each proposal voted on at the meeting are set forth below:

Election of Directors. The Companys shareholders elected four directors to serve until the next annual meeting of shareholders and the election of their successors. The number of votes cast for each of the directors is set forth below. There were 772,700 broker non-votes.
