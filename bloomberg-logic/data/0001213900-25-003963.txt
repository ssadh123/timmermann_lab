Item 8.01 Other Events.

As previously disclosed, on October 24, 2024,
BM Technologies, Inc., a Delaware Corporation (the Company), entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (the Merger
Agreement) with First Carolina Bank, a North Carolina state-chartered bank (Parent), and Double Eagle Acquisition
Corp., Inc., a Delaware corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent (Merger Sub). The Merger Agreement provides
for the merger of Merger Sub with and into the Company (the Merger), with the Company surviving the Merger as a wholly owned
subsidiary of Parent.

The Company, Parent and Merger Sub have
agreed that the closing date of the Merger will be January 31, 2025, and the effective time of the Merger will be 11:59 p.m.,
Eastern Time on January 31, 2025 (subject to the waiver (to the extent permitted by law) or satisfaction of the required closing
conditions).
