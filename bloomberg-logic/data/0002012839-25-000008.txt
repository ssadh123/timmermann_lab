Item 3.02 Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities.

On February 1, 2025, the KKR Enhanced US Direct Lending Fund-L Inc. (the Company) issued and sold 19,214 shares of the Companys common shares of beneficial interest (the Shares) (with the final number of common shares issued being determined on February 18, 2025) pursuant to a subscription agreement entered into with KKR Enhanced US Direct Lending Fund-L Holdings L.P. (the Subscriber) for aggregate consideration of $20.00 million.

The offer and sale of the Shares were conducted in connection with the Companys continuous private offering of Shares (the Private Offering) in reliance on the exemption from the registration requirements of the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the Securities Act), provided by Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act, Regulation D promulgated thereunder and/or Regulation S under the Securities Act.
