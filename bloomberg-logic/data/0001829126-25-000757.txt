Item 8.01. Other Events.

On
February 3, 2025, the Registration Statement on Form S-1 (the Registration Statement) filed by Nexalin Technology,
Inc. (the Company) was declared effective by the Securities and Exchange Commission. The Registration Statement covers
up to 2,798,613 shares of the Companys common stock, consisting of (i) up to 347,250 Shares issuable upon the exercise of the
common stock purchase warrant that was issued to Maxim Partners, LLC (Maxim Partners) as compensation for its services
in connection with the Companys 2022 initial public offering; (ii) up to 2,315,000 Shares issuable upon the exercise of the
common stock purchase warrants issued to purchasers of Units (containing both a share of common stock and a common stock purchase
warrant) in connection the Companys 2022 public offering; and (iii) 136,363 shares of common stock issued to Maxim Partners
pursuant to a renewal of its engagement agreement with the Company. All warrants are exercisable at a price of $4.15 per share of
the Companys common stock.
