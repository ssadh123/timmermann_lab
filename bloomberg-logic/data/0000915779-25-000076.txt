Section 7 - Regulation FD

Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure. 

On March 4, 2025, the Board of Directors of Daktronics, Inc. (the Company) voted to authorize an additional $10 million of its outstanding shares of common stock under the stock buyback program. As of March 4, 2025, approximately $20 million remained available under the program for the repurchase of shares. 

Shares may be repurchased from time to time in open market purchases, private transactions or other transactions. The timing, volume and nature of share repurchases will be at the sole discretion of management and will be dependent on market conditions, applicable securities laws and other factors, and may be suspended or discontinued at any time. Under the stock repurchase program, the Company may repurchase shares in open-market purchases in accordance with all applicable securities laws and regulations, including Rule 10b-18 under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended. No assurance can be given that any particular number of shares of common stock will be repurchased.
