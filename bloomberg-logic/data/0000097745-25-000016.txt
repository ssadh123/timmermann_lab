Item 8.01 Other Events

On February 25, 2025, Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc. (Thermo Fisher) and Solventum Corporation (Solventum) entered into an agreement, pursuant to which, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of specified conditions, Thermo Fisher agreed to acquire Solventums purification and filtration business for approximately $4.1 billion in cash. The transaction is expected to be completed by the end of 2025 and is subject to customary closing conditions and regulatory approvals.
