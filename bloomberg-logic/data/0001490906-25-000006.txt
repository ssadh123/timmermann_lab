Item 5.07 SUBMISSION OF MATTERS TO A VOTE OF SECURITY HOLDERS 

Capitol Federal Financial, Inc. (the Company) held its Annual Meeting of Stockholders on January 28, 2025 (the Annual Meeting). Holders of record of the Companys common stock at the close of business on December 6, 2024 were entitled to vote on four items at the Annual Meeting. Stockholders elected John B. Dicus, James G. Morris, and Jeffrey R. Thompson each to a three-year term as director. The stockholders approved, on an advisory basis, the compensation of the Companys named executive officers, as disclosed in the Companys proxy statement for the Annual Meeting (the "Say on Pay Vote"). The stockholders ratified the appointment of KPMG LLP as the Companys independent auditors for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2025. Stockholders did not approve an amendment of the Company's charter to declassify the Board of Directors. The final voting results of each item are set forth below.
