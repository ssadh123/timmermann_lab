Item 3.02 Unregistered Sale of Equity Securities

On April 1, 2025, Ares Core Infrastructure Fund (the Fund) sold common shares of beneficial interest (the Common Shares) for an aggregate purchase price of $84.4 million. The number of Common Shares to be issued was finalized on April 22, 2025. The purchase price per Common Share equaled the Funds net asset value (NAV) per Common Share as of March 31, 2025. The issuance of the Common Shares is exempt from the registration requirements of the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, pursuant to Section 4(a)(2) thereof and Rule 506(b) of Regulation D thereunder. The following table details the Common Shares sold:

Date of Unregistered Sales Amount of Common Shares Total Consideration (in millions)
