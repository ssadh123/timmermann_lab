PRESS RELEASE FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

January 17, 2025

Five Star Bancorp Declares Fourth Quarter Cash Dividend

RANCHO CORDOVA, CA January 17, 2025 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) Five Star Bancorp (Nasdaq: FSBC) (Five Star or the Company), a holding company that operates through its wholly owned banking subsidiary, Five Star Bank (the "Bank"), announced today the declaration of a cash dividend of $0.20 per share on the Companys voting common stock. The dividend is expected to be paid on February 10, 2025, to shareholders of record as of February 3, 2025.

About Five Star Bancorp

Five Star is a bank holding company headquartered in Rancho Cordova, California. Five Star operates through its wholly owned banking subsidiary, Five Star Bank. The Bank has eight branches in Northern California. For more information, visit https://www.fivestarbank.com.
