Item 5.07 

Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders

On February 4, 2025, PennantPark Investment Corporation (the Company) held its annual meeting of stockholders (the Annual Meeting) and submitted two matters to the vote of stockholders. The proposals are described in detail in the Companys definitive proxy statement filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on December 11, 2024. As of December 4, 2024, the record date, 65,296,094 shares of common stock were eligible to vote. A summary of the matters voted upon by the stockholders is set forth below.

Proposal 1. The Companys stockholders elected two Class III directors of the Company, who will each serve until the 2028 Annual Meeting and until his successor is duly elected and qualifies.
