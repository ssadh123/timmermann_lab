Item 8.01. 

Other Events.

On January 13, 2025, Elevation Oncology, Inc. (the Company) issued a press release announcing program updates and upcoming milestones. The Company announced that, with respect to its ongoing Phase 1 clinical trial of EO-3021, a Claudin 18.2 antibody-drug conjugate (ADC): (i) patient dosing is ongoing in the combination portion of the trial, which evaluates EO-3021 in combination with dostarlimab, a PD-1 inhibitor, in the first line setting and with ramucirumab, a VEGFR2 inhibitor, in the second line setting; (ii) the Company expects to report initial data from the combination cohorts in the fourth quarter of 2025 or the first quarter of 2026; (iii) as of January 2025, the Company has implemented prospective Claudin 18.2 expression testing as part of the patient screening process for the monotherapy dose expansion portion, focusing enrollment on patients with 25% of tumor cells at IHC 1+/2+/3+; and (iv) the Company plans to report additional safety and efficacy data from the monotherapy dose escalation and expansion portions in the first half of 2025.

The Company also announced that, with respect to EO-1022, a HER3 ADC, the Company expects to present preclinical data in the first half of 2025 and to file an Investigational New Drug application in 2026. In addition, the Company announced that it expects that its cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities as of September 30, 2024 will be sufficient to fund its current operations into 2026.
