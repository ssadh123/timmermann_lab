Employment Agreement with Victoria M. Mink 

On January 6, 2025, Madison Square Garden Sports Corp. (the Company) and Victoria M. Mink, the Executive Vice President, Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer of the Company, entered into an employment agreement (the Employment Agreement), effective as of January 1, 2025. The Employment Agreement is substantially the same as the existing employment agreement between the Company and Ms. Mink, except that (i) the annual base salary will be not less than $1,000,000, (ii) it is expected that Ms. Mink will receive annual grants of cash and/or equity long-term incentive awards with an aggregate target value of not less than $1,500,000 as determined by the Compensation Committee of the Board in its discretion and (iii) the scheduled expiration date of the Employment Agreement is January 1, 2028. 

The foregoing description of the Employment Agreement does not purport to be complete and is subject to, and qualified in its entirety by, the full text of the Employment Agreement, a copy of which is filed as Exhibit 10.1, and incorporated herein by reference.
