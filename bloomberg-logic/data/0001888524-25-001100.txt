Item 6.04 Failure to Make a Required Distribution.

The Certificate Administrator
made distributions to the Certificateholders on the December 12, 2024
Distribution Date and the January 10, 2024 Distribution Date. The
Certificate Administrator was subsequently notified by the Master Servicer on
January 24, 2025 of receipt of funds from the late payoff of the Academy Sports
Evansville Mortgage Loan (Loan Number 41 on Annex A-1 of the prospectus
supplement of the registrant relating to the issuing entity filed on February
3, 2015 pursuant to Rule 424(b)(5)). 

This resulted in an
underpayment of principal to the Class A-4 Certificateholders in an amount
equal to $6,300,000.00 for the December distribution. 

The late payoff described above
also resulted in the following changes to the January distribution:

an overpayment of principal to the Class
A-4 Certificateholders in an amount equal to $6,300,000.00 and an overpayment
of interest to the Class A-4 Certificateholders in an amount equal to
$16,710.75;

an underpayment of principal to the Class
A-M Certificatolders in an amount equal to $6,292,806.92;

an overpayment of interest to the Class C
Certificateholders in an amount equal to $287.70;

an overpayment of interest to the Class E
Certificateholders in an amount equal to $147.87;

an overpayment of interest to the Class F
Certificateholders in an amount equal to $62.26;

an overpayment of interest to the Class G
Certificateholders in an amount equal to $63.63;

an overpayment of interest to the Class H
Certificateholders in an amount equal to $173.49;

an overpayment of interest to the Class
X-A Certificateholders in an amount equal to $6,343.74;

an overpayment of interest to the Class
X-B Certificateholders in an amount equal to $496.36; and

an overpayment of interest to the Class
X-C Certificateholders in an amount equal to $309.11.

The
revised Monthly Distribution Reports are included with the registrants Form 10-D/As
for the December 2024 and January 2025 distribution periods filed on the date
of this Form 8-K. All of the payment adjustments described above for the
December 2024 and January 2025 distribution periods are expected to be made on
or prior to the next distribution date.
