Item 1.01. Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

Convertible Loan Agreement

On December 31, 2024, NKGen Biotech, Inc. (the
Company) entered into a convertible loan agreement (the Loan Agreement), by and among the Company,
NKGen Operating Biotech, Inc. (NKGen Operating and together with the Company, the Borrowers)
and AlpineBrook Capital GP I Limited (the Lender). The Loan Agreement provides for term loan financing in a principal
amount of up to $4.5 million (the Term Loan) with $4,180,000 cash advanced prior to the closing. The Term Loan bears
interest at a rate of 12% per annum (the Applicable Rate), accruing from the dates on which the Term Loan, in one
or more installments, was, or is, actually made available by the Lender to the Borrowers. Pursuant to the Loan Agreement, the Borrowers
will cause any subsidiaries of the Borrowers formed or acquired during such time as the Term Loan is outstanding, to guarantee and/or
become jointly and severally liable for the obligations of the Borrowers under the Loan Agreement.

Note

On December 31, 2024, pursuant to the Loan Agreement,
and evidencing the Term Loan as described above, the Borrowers issued to the Lender a convertible promissory note (the Note)
in the principal amount of up to $4.5 million which bears interest at the Applicable Rate. The Note matures on December 31, 2025 (the
Maturity Date). At any time from the issue date to the Maturity Date, and upon at least 61 days notice, the
Lender has the right to convert all or any portion of the then outstanding and unpaid principal amount of the Note and all accrued and
unpaid interest into shares of the Companys common stock, par value $0.0001 (the Common Stock), at a conversion
price of $0.25 per share (subject to adjustments and limitations as described in the Note).

Consideration Shares and Warrant

Pursuant to the Loan Agreement, 1.5 million shares
of Common Stock and a warrant to purchase 1.5 million shares of Common Stock (the Warrant) were issued to the Lender
as additional consideration for the Term Loan. The Warrant has a five-year term and an exercise price of $0.25 per share (subject to adjustments
and limitations as described in the Warrant).

Shareholder Approval

Pursuant to the Loan Agreement, the Company agreed
to seek stockholder approval of the issuance of Common Stock in excess of the Exchange Cap (as defined below). Until such approval is
obtained, the Company will not issue shares of Common Stock to the Lender to the extent such issuance would cause the total number of
shares issued to exceed the limits set under Nasdaq Listing Rule 5635(d) (the Exchange Cap).

The foregoing descriptions of the Warrant, Loan
Agreement and Note do not purport to be complete and are qualified in their entirety by the terms and conditions of the Warrant, Loan
Agreement and Note which are filed as Exhibits 4.1, 10.1 and 10.2, respectively, to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein
by reference.
