Item 5.07. Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

On February 3, 2025, Golub Capital Direct Lending Unlevered Corporation (the Company) held its annual meeting of stockholders (the Annual Meeting). At the Annual Meeting, the Companys stockholders approved two proposals. The issued and outstanding shares of stock of the Company entitled to vote at the Annual Meeting consisted of 21,548,001 shares of common stock outstanding on the record date, December 5, 2024. The final voting results from the Annual Meeting were as follows:

Proposal 1. To elect three Class III directors of the Company who will each serve until the 2028 Annual Meeting of Stockholders of the Company or until his or her successor is duly elected and qualified.
