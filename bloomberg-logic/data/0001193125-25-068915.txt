Item 8.01. Other Events. 

On March 28, 2025, the Dividend Committee, as delegated by the Board of Trustees of Manulife Private Credit Fund (the Fund), declared a quarterly dividend of $0.38326 per share on the Funds common shares of beneficial interest (the Shares), which will be paid on or about April 22, 2025 to shareholders of record as of the close of business on March 27, 2025.
