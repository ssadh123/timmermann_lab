(b) On April 8, 2025, Christopher Wright notified Roper Technologies, Inc. (the Company) of his intention to retire from the Companys Board of Directors, effective as of the Companys 2025 Annual Meeting of Shareholders to be held on June 10, 2025. Mr. Wright has served as a Director of the Company since 1991. The size of the Companys Board of Directors will be reduced to 9 directors effective upon Mr. Wrights retirement. Mr. Wrights decision to retire was not due to any disagreements with the Company on any matter relating to the Companys operations, policies, or practices.
