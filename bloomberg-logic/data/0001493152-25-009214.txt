Item
5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of
Certain Officers.

On
February 28, 2025, each of Charles Benton and Judd Brammah notified Vision Energy Corporation (the Company) of his resignation
as a director of the Company, effective immediately. Mr. Bentons and Mr. Brammahs resignations are not due to any disagreement
with the Company or any matter relating to the Companys operations, policies, or practices. The Company thanks Mr. Benton and
Mr. Brammah for their contributions as members of its Board.

Also
on February 28, 2025, the Company paid Mr. Brammah $118,557.20 in exchange for cancellation of 1,185,572 shares of common stock of the
Company held by Mr. Brammah, constituting all of his ownership of securities of the Company.
