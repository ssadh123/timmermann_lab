Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

Hancock Whitney Corporation (the Company) held its 2025 annual meeting of shareholders (the Annual Meeting) on April 23, 2025 in Gulfport, Mississippi. The Annual Meeting was held in a virtual-only format.

At the close of business on February 28, 2025, the record date for the Annual Meeting, the Company had 86,127,857 shares of common stock outstanding and entitled to vote. Of that number, 76,546,747shares of common stock were represented virtually or by proxy at the Annual Meeting. The Companys shareholders voted on the following four proposals at the Annual Meeting, casting their votes as described below.
