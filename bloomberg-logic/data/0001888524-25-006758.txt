Item 6.04 Failure to Make a Required Distribution.

The
Certificate Administrator made distributions to the Certificateholders on the
March 17, 2025 Distribution Date. The Certificate Administrator was
subsequently notified by the Master Servicer on April 14, 2025 of receipt of
funds from the liquidation of the Michigan Rite Aid Portfolio Mortgage Loan
(Loan Number 52 on Annex A-1 of the prospectus of the registrant relating to
the issuing entity filed on December 21, 2016 pursuant to Rule
424(b)(2)). This resulted in an additional principal payment to the Class
A-SB Certificateholders in an amount equal to $1,786,937.09, an additional
payment to the Class A-SB Certificateholders in an amount equal to $19,205.19
in prepayment penalties, an additional interest payment to the Class G
Certificateholders in an amount equal to $14,562.50 and an additional payment
to the Class X-A Certificateholders in an amount equal to $8,886.63 in
prepayment penalties. The additional payments are expected to be made to
the Class A-SB, Class G and Class X-A Certificateholders on or prior to the
next distribution date.
