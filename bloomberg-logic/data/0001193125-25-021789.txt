Tammy M. Finley, the Companys Executive Vice President, General Counsel and Corporate Secretary, will be leaving the organization and beginning her retirement. Effective March 2, 2025, Ms. Finley is expected to serve in an advisory capacity through May 2, 2025, to support the transition of the role. Ms. Finleys separation triggered termination without due cause payment pursuant to her employment agreement with the Company. The Company thanks Ms. Finley for her more than 25 years of service in both the legal and human resources functions.
