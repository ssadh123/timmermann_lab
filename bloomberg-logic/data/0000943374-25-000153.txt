Item 5.07Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

On April 15, 2025, ESSA Bancorp, Inc. (the Company) held a Special Meeting of Shareholders (the Special Meeting). At the close of business on the
record date for the Special Meeting, there were a total of 10,154,664 shares of Company common stock outstanding and entitled to vote at the Special Meeting. At the Special Meeting, 8,856,349 shares of common stock were represented, therefore a
quorum was present. At the Special Meeting, the Companys shareholders voted on three proposals, as more specifically described in the proxy statement/prospectus of the Company and CNB Financial Corporation (CNB), dated March 5, 2025 (the proxy
statement/prospectus). Set forth below are the final results of shareholder votes for all proposals.

Proposal 1 Merger Proposal

A proposal to approve the Agreement and Plan of Merger, by and among CNB, CNB
Bank, the Company, and ESSA Bank & Trust (ESSA Bank), dated as of January 9, 2025, pursuant to which (i) the Company will merge with and into CNB, with CNB as the surviving entity, and (ii) ESSA Bank will merge with and into CNB Bank, with
CNB Bank as the surviving entity, was approved by the affirmative vote of a majority of the votes cast by shareholders at the Special Meeting, as indicated below:
