Item 4.01 Change in Registrants Certifying
Accountant.

(b) Engagement of New Independent Registered
Public Accounting Firm.

On January 15, 2025, Bloom HoldCo LLC (the Company)
engaged CBIZ CPAs P.C. (CBIZ) as the Companys independent registered public accounting firm to audit the Companys
consolidated financial statements for the year ending September 30, 2024.

During the period from September 30, 2022 through
September 30, 2024, neither the Company, nor any party on behalf of the Company, consulted CBIZ regarding either (i) the application of
accounting principles to a specified transaction, either completed or proposed, or the type of audit opinion that might be rendered on
the Companys financial statements, and no written report or oral advice was provided to the Company by CBIZ that was an important
factor considered by the Company in reaching a decision as to any accounting, auditing or financial reporting issue, or (ii) any matter
that was either the subject of a disagreement, as defined in Item 304(a)(1)(iv) of Regulation S-K, or a reportable
event, as defined in Item 304(a)(1)(v) of Regulations S-K.
