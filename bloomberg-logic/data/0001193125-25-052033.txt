Item 5.07 

Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders. 

Universal Technical Institute, Inc (the Company) held its Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the Annual Meeting) on March 6, 2025. At the Annual Meeting, the Companys stockholders voted on three proposals. The proposals are described in the Companys definitive proxy statement on Schedule 14A filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on January 15, 2025. The voting results for each of the proposals are set forth below. 

Proposal 1:The stockholders elected each of the four nominees as Class III Directors to serve a three-year term ending in 2028, or until the Directors successor is duly elected and qualified, as follows: 

Director Votes For Votes Against Abstentions Broker Non-Votes
