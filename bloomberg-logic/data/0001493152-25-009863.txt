Item
1.01 Entry into a Material Agreement

On
March 15, 2025, Earth Science Tech, Inc., a Florida corporation (the Company), amended its January 30, 2025, Acquisition
Agreement with Las Villas Healthcare, LLC and Doconsultations.com, LLC (the Targets). The amendment reduces the purchase
price from four hundred thousand dollars to two hundred thousand dollars. Under the terms of the original agreement, the Targets had
already received an initial payment of fifty thousand dollars. The remaining balance of one hundred fifty thousand dollars will be paid
at closing, which is scheduled to occur following the completion of the ongoing ninety-day due diligence period.
