DA-1241 Phase 2a: Clinical Study
A MULTICENTER, RANDOMIZED, DOUBLE-BLIND, PLACEBO-CONTROLLED, PARALLEL, PHASE 2A CLINICAL TRIAL
TO EVALUATE THE EFFICACY AND SAFETY OF DA-1241 IN SUBJECTS WITH PRESUMED NON-ALCOHOLIC
STEATOHEPATITIS (NASH)
Objective
Part 1: To explore the efficacy (change from baseline in ALT) of DA-1241 in subjects at risk of NASH or
subjects with non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD) after administration of oral DA-1241 at varying
doses or identical placebo for 16 weeks/112 days.
Part 2: To explore the efficacy (change from baseline in ALT) of DA-1241 in subjects at risk of NASH or
subjects with NAFLD after administration of oral DA-1241 in combination with sitagliptin versus
identical placebo for 16 weeks/112 days.
