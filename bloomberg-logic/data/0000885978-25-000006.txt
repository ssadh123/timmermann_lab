ITEM 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure 

On March 7, 2025 U.S. Physical Therapy, Inc. (the Company) (NYSE: USPH), a
national operator of outpatient physical therapy clinics and provider of industrial injury prevention services, updated its investor presentation on the Companys website at www.usph.com. The presentation covers an overview of the Company.
