Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure.

JetBlue Airways Corporation ("JetBlue" or the "Company") announced today an operational and financial update of its expected first quarter 2025 results.

Through the first two months of 2025, specifically in February, JetBlue's operation faced more weather-related disruptions than in the prior year. While this impacted completion factor in the quarter, the Company's continued focus on operational reliability helped offset the potential impact of significant disruption costs. 

In addition to impacts from weather, JetBlue is also experiencing demand choppiness due to mixed macroeconomic indicators, resulting in trough period revenue performing below expectations in the first quarter. JetBlue actioned a targeted capacity reduction in March and April, focused on off-peak day-of-week adjustments, and the Company continues to monitor trough performance in April and May. 

Demand for travel during peak periods remains healthy, and JetBlue continues to be pleased by the progress of its JetForward initiatives.
