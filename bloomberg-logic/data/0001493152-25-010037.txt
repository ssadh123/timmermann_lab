Item
1.01. Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement

On
May 20, 2024, Globalink Investment Inc. a Delaware corporation (the Company or Globalink), Alps Global Holding
Pubco, a Cayman Islands exempted company (PubCo), Alps Biosciences Merger Sub, a Cayman Islands exempted company and wholly-owned
subsidiary of PubCo (Merger Sub), Alps Life Sciences Inc., a Cayman Islands company (Alps Holdco), GL Sponsor
LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (Parent Representative), and Dr. Tham Seng Kong, an individual, in the capacity
as the representative for the shareholders of Alps Holdco (Seller Representative), entered into an amended and restated
merger agreement, as amended on March 6, 2025 (the Amended and Restated Merger Agreement).

As
of the date of this current report on Form 8-K, Globalink has issued an aggregate of US$4.39 million in promissory notes (the Promissory
Notes) to Public Gold Marketing Sdn. Bhd., a company incorporated in Malaysia and an affiliate of the sponsor of Globalink (PGM).

On
March 7, 2025, Globalink, PubCo and PGM entered into an agreement, pursuant to which the parties agreed that US$2 million of the outstanding
balance under the Promissory Notes shall be due and payable in cash within 60 days from the date of the closing of the transactions (the
Transactions) contemplated by the Amended and Restated Merger Agreement (the Closing), and the remaining
balance under the Promissory Notes shall be converted into ordinary shares of PubCo at the time of Closing, at a conversion price of
US$10.00 per share.

The
foregoing description of the Agreement is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the form of Amendment, a copy of
which is filed as Exhibit 1.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein by reference.
