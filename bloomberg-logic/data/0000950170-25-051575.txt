Item 8.01 Other Events

On April 7, 2025, Atossa Therapeutics, Inc. (the Company) announced that Intas Pharmaceuticals Ltd. filed a Petition for Post Grant Review (PGR) with the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office (the PGR Petition) relating to one of the Companys issued patents (U.S. Patent No. 12,071,391), titled, Methods for Making and Using Endoxifen, and also filed a Petition for Inter Partes Review (IPR) with the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office (the IPR Petition) relating to one of the Companys issued patents (U.S. Patent No. 11,261,151), titled Methods for Making and Using Endoxifen, (together with U.S. Patent No. 12,071,391, the Patents). The Company intends to vigorously contest the PGR and IPR Petitions and believes that the Patents were properly granted and include valid and enforceable claims. However, there can be no assurance that the Company will prevail in contesting the PGR and IPR Petitions.
