Section 8 Other Events

Item 8.01 Other Events.

Effective as of March 13, 2025, Green Loan Services LLC (GLS), a Delaware limited liability company, will act as special servicer for the 237 Park Avenue mortgage loan and each related pari passu and/or subordinate promissory note (collectively, the 237 Park Avenue Non-Serviced Loan Combination), which is serviced under the trust and servicing agreement for the MSSG 2017-237P
securitization (the MSSG 2017-237P TSA), replacing LNR Partners, LLC as special servicer for such non-serviced loan combination. GLS was appointed at the direction of 237 P Holdings LLC, the directing certificateholder under the MSSG 2017-237P TSA. As special servicer for the 237 Park Avenue Non-Serviced Loan Combination, GLS will be responsible for the servicing and administration of the 237 Park Avenue Non-Serviced Loan Combination if it becomes specially serviced (and the servicing
and administration of any related REO property) and processing and performing certain reviews of material actions with respect to the 237 Park Avenue Non-Serviced Loan Combination when the 237 Park Avenue Non-Serviced Loan Combination is not specially serviced. Servicing of the 237 Park Avenue Non-Serviced Loan Combination will continue to be governed by the MSSG 2017-237P TSA.
