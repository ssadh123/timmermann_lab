Item 8.01 

Other Events. 

On March 31, 2025, Atea Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (the Company or Atea) received notice from Driver Opportunity Partners III withdrawing its prior notice that it intended to nominate J. Abbott R. Cooper to stand for election to the Companys Board of Directors at the Companys 2025 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the 2025 Annual Meeting). As a result, J. Abbott R. Cooper will not stand for election at the 2025 Annual Meeting.
