Item 7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure

As previously disclosed, on December 18, 2023, United States Steel Corporation (the Company) entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (such agreement, as it may be amended, modified or supplemented from time to time, the Merger Agreement) by and among the Company, Nippon Steel North America, Inc., a New York corporation (Parent), 2023 Merger Subsidiary, Inc., a Delaware corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent (Merger Sub), and solely as provided in Section 9.13 therein, Nippon Steel Corporation, a Japanese corporation (NSC). Pursuant to the Merger Agreement, and upon the terms and subject to the conditions described therein, Merger Sub will merge with and into the Company, with the Company continuing as the surviving corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent (the Merger). Also as previously disclosed, on January 3, 2025, the President of the United States issued an executive order prohibiting the Merger (the Executive Order).

Section 2(b) of the Executive Order required the Company and NSC to abandon the Merger Agreement within thirty (30) days. On January 10, 2025, the Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States granted an extension of that deadline to June 18, 2025.

In accordance with General Instruction B.2 of Form 8-K, the information contained in Item 7.01 is being furnished and shall not be deemed filed for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the Exchange Act), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that section, nor shall such information be incorporated by reference into any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in such a filing.
