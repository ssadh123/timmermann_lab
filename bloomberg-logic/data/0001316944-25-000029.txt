Item 5.02. Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

This Form 8-K/A amends the Form 8-K filed by the Federal Home Loan Bank of San Francisco (Bank) on November 15, 2024, to add information about the Committees of the Banks Board of Directors (Board) on which Directors Jeffrey K. Ball, Lori R. Gay, and F. Daniel Siciliano will serve in 2025.

The Board has appointed Mr. Ball, Ms. Gay, and Mr. Siciliano to the Boards Committees as follows:

Jeffrey K. Ball Chair of the Regulatory Compliance Oversight Committee, Vice Chair of the Audit Committee, Member of the Affordable Housing and Community Investment, Diversity Equity and Inclusion, and Public Affairs Committees.

Lori R. Gay Chair of the Affordable Housing and Community Investment Committee, and Member of the Diversity Equity and Inclusion, Governance, and Public Affairs Committees.

F. Daniel Siciliano Chair of the Board of Directors, and Member of the Diversity Equity and Inclusion Committee.
