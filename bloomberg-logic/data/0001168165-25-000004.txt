Section 2 Financial Information

Item 2.03. Creation of a Direct Financial Obligation or an Obligation under an Off-Balance Sheet Arrangement of a Registrant.

On December 31, 2024, wholly owned subsidiaries of Constellation Energy Generation, LLC (Constellation), Constellation NewEnergy, Inc. (CNE), as servicer, and NewEnergy Receivables LLC (NER), a bankruptcy-remote, special purpose entity wholly owned by CNE, as seller, entered into a Receivables Financing Agreement with MUFG Bank, LTD., as Agent, the Lenders party thereto, the Arrangers party thereto and the Group Agents party thereto (the Receivables Financing Agreement). The Receivables Financing Agreement amends and restates a Receivables Purchase Agreement originally entered into by the parties thereto on April 8, 2020.

The Receivables Financing Agreement, among other things, extends the term of the facility through December 31, 2027, unless further extended as set forth therein, and increases the maximum funding limit of the facility from $1.1 billion to $1.5 billion. No additional funds were drawn in connection with the Receivables Financing Agreement. The Receivables Financing Agreement is subject to covenants and amortization events that are customary for transactions of this type.
