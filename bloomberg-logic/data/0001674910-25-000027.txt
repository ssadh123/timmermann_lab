Item 5.07. Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

On January 28, 2025, Valvoline Inc. (Valvoline) held its Annual Meeting of Shareholders (2025 Annual Meeting). At the 2025 Annual Meeting, a total of 119,205,736 shares of Valvoline common stock, representing 93% of the shares outstanding and eligible to vote and constituting a quorum, were represented in person or by valid proxies. The final results for each of the matters submitted to a vote of shareholders at the 2025 Annual Meeting are as follows:
