Item 8.01 Other Events.

On March
17, 2025, the Company entered into a Purchase Plan Agreement authorizing a share repurchase program of up to $2.0 million of the Companys
common stock. Repurchases will be made through open market purchases, block trades and/or privately negotiated transactions in compliance
with Rule 10b-18 and Rule 10b5-1 promulgated under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, subject to market conditions,
applicable legal requirements, and other relevant factors.

The timing,
volume and nature of the repurchases will be at the discretion of management based on its evaluation of the capital needs of the Company,
market conditions, applicable legal requirements and other factors. There is no guarantee as to the number of shares that will be repurchased,
if any. Repurchases under the share repurchase program are expected to be funded from existing cash balances. The repurchase program expires
on March 7, 2026, and may be extended, suspended, modified or discontinued at any time at the Companys discretion.
