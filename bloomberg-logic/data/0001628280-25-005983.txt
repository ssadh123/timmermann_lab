Item 8.01 Other Events.

Effective February 18, 2025, Dr. Veronique Lecault has transitioned from Chief Operating Officer (COO) to Chief Technology Officer (CTO).The change in Dr. Lecaults title was mutually agreed upon and was not due to any disagreement with the Company on any matter relating to its operations, policies, or practices.Dr. Lecaults roles and responsibilities as COO will transfer to her role as CTO.Dr. Lecault will continue as a Director on the Companys Board of Directors.
