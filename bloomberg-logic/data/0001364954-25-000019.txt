Item 8.01 Other Events

On March 3, 2025 and March 4, 2025, Chegg, Inc. (the Company) entered into additional individual, privately negotiated repurchase agreements with certain holders of its outstanding 0% Convertible Senior Notes due 2026 (the 2026 Notes) to repurchase approximately $8.6 million in aggregate principal amount of the 2026 Notes for an aggregate cash repurchase price of approximately $7.6 million. Together with the repurchase of $56.6 million aggregate principal amount of the 2026 Notes for an aggregate cash repurchase price of $49.8 million as disclosed in the Current Report on Form 8-K previously filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on March 3, 2025, the total aggregate principal amount of the 2026 Notes repurchased was $65.2 million for an aggregate cash repurchase price of $57.4 million (the notes repurchase transactions). 

The notes repurchase transactions were entered into in connection with our previously announced securities repurchase program and are expected to close on March 7, 2025, subject to the satisfaction of customary closing conditions. Following the closing, approximately $62.7 million aggregate principal amount of the 2026 Notes will remain outstanding and $150.1 million will remain available under our securities repurchase program.
