Item 1.01 

Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

As previously disclosed, pursuant to an Interim Concession Agreement (the Concession Agreement) between Saker Aviation Services, Inc. (the Company) and the New York City Economic Development Corporation (NYCEDC), the Company has the exclusive right to operate as the fixed base operator for the Downtown Manhattan Heliport located at Pier 6 in Manhattan (the Heliport) until June 12, 2025, subject to early termination.

On March 4, 2025, the Company was notified by NYCEDC that NYCEDC will be terminating the Concession Agreement effective March 29, 2025. Pursuant to the termination the Company must vacate and cease use of the Heliport by March 29, 2025.

Operation of the Heliport is the Companys only revenue generating business.
