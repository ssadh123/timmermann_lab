Atlanta, April 9, 20251 --- Invesco Ltd. (NYSE: IVZ)1 today reported preliminary month-end assets under management (AUM) of $1,844.8 billion, a decrease of 2.3% versus previous month-end. The firm delivered net long-term inflows of $6.5 billion in the month. Non-management fee earning net outflows were $1.7 billion and money market net inflows were $5.8 billion. AUM was negatively impacted by unfavorable market returns which decreased AUM by $60 billion. FX and reinvested distributions increased AUM by $5.2 billion. Preliminary average total AUM for the quarter through March 31 were $1,880.8 billion, and preliminary average active AUM for the quarter through March 31 were $1,043.1 billion.
