Item 1.02 Termination of a Material Definitive Agreement.

On March 4, 2025, Lake Shore, MHC and Lake Shore Bancorp, Inc. (collectively, the "Companies"), the parent savings and loan holding companies of Lake Shore Savings Bank, received a notification from the Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia (the Reserve Bank), the Companies' regulator, terminating the written agreement, dated June 28, 2023 between the Reserve Bank and the Companies.

The terms of the former written agreement were previously disclosed on a Current Report on Form 8-K, dated June 28, 2023.
