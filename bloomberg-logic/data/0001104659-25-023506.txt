Item 5.07. Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

At the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders of
TE Connectivity plc (the Company) held on March 12, 2025 (the AGM), shareholders voted on the agenda items
described below.

Proxies for the AGM were solicited pursuant to
Regulation 14A under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, and there was no solicitation in opposition to the Companys
solicitation.

A total of 271,942,700 ordinary shares (91.1% of
298,472,685 ordinary shares outstanding and entitled to vote as of January 13, 2025, the record date for the AGM) were present by proxy,
