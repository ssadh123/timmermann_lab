Issuance of Common

In connection with the
foregoing, the Company relied upon the exemption from registration provided under Section 4(a)(2) under the Securities Act for transactions

Issuance of Common

Company issued an aggregate of 14,130,851,121 shares of common stock of the Company to investors pursuant to Convertible Promissory Note
Conversion Agreements.

foregoing, the Company relied upon the exemption from registration provided under Section 4(a)(2) under the Securities Act for transactions
not involving a public offering.

Issuance of Common Stock

On March 31, 2025, the Company issued 17,291,904
shares of the Companys common stock to a former executive as a management buy-out payment and another 1,714,286 shares of common
stock to another individual as compensation for services.

In connection with the foregoing, the Company relied upon the exemption
from registration provided under Section 4(a)(2) under the Securities Act for transactions not involving a public offering.

Dividends in Shares
of Common Stock

Company issued an aggregate of 840,912 shares of the Companys common stock as dividends to certain holders of Series O preferred

not involving a public offering.
