Introductory Note

We are filing this Current Report on Amendment No. 1 of the Form 8-K/A to supplement Item 7.01 of the Report regarding the preliminary findings from the National Center for Asphalt Technology (NCAT) on the Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) of the Company's test section S11 at the NCAT Test Track which was originally filed on Form 8-K to SEC on March 14, 2025.

Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure

On March 13, 2025, the company issued a Press Release announcing groundbreaking preliminary findings from the National Center for Asphalt Technology (NCAT) regarding the Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) of its test section S11 at the NCAT Test Track. The findings demonstrate the profound impact of the company's TerraZyme technology in road construction, showcasing a remarkable 46% reduction in overall carbon emissions. NCAT conducted an analysis comparing the company's TerraZyme technology used on test section S11, with traditional soil stabilization methods employed by a State Department of Transportation on test section S2.

A supplemental summary of the Simplified Preliminary LCA Results on the performance of NCAT test track sections S2 and S11 is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this filing.
