ITEM 8.01 Other Events.

Under the Fourth Amended and Restated
Limited Liability Company Agreement, dated January 14, 2019 (the Agreement), of TCW Direct Lending VII LLC (the Company),
the Company has an initial six-year term following its initial closing. Under Section 8.1 of the Agreement, the Company may, by notice
to its members, extend the term of the Company for two additional one-year periods after its initial six-year term. By notice dated January
3, 2025, the Company has extended its term by one year such that it will expire on April 13, 2026.
