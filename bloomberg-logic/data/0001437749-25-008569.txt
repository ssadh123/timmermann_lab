Item 8.01 Other Events

On March 19, 2025, Investar Holding Corporation (the Company) received $3.4 million as a property insurance settlement related to a loan relationship that became impaired in the third quarter of 2021 as a result of Hurricane Ida. The estimated pre-tax impact to the Companys net income will be an addition of $3.1 million for the three months ending March 31, 2025, which reflects the settlement amount less estimated expenses of $0.3 million. The funds received in the settlement will be recognized in the Companys consolidated financial statements as a recovery of loans previously charged off. At March 20, 2025, the Companys other real estate owned related to this relationship included two remaining properties with a total cost basis of $1.7 million, which the Company is actively marketing for sale. Upon sale of these properties, the Company will have arrived at final resolution of this loan relationship.
