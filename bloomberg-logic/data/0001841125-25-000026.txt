INTRODUCTIONWHO IS BOLT PROJECTS HOLDINGS? 4 BOLT IS PIONEERING A NEW GENERATION OF BIOTECH INGREDIENTS FOR THE BEAUTY AND PERSONAL CARE INDUSTRY 

OUR PLATFORM A collection of patented materials built on shared technology and functional benefits, designed to streamline development and offer versatile solutions to common industry challenges. Made through fermentation, our vegan silks are collection complex polypeptides that act as a lightweight, film-forming alternative to silicone elastomers a commonly used ingredient under increasing regulatory and consumer scrutiny due to concerns about their impact on human health and the environment. Our platform of vegan silks deliver 20+ clinically-proven benefits, are fully bio-based, and biodegradable, providing formulators the ability to incorporate them into products without trading efficacy for regulatory compliance.
