Section 8 Other Events

Item 8.01 Other Events.

Effective as of March 4, 2025, Midland Loan Services, a Division of PNC Bank, National Association (Midland), a national banking association, will act as special servicer for the Boca Office Portfolio mortgage loan and each related pari passu and/or subordinate promissory note (collectively, the Boca Office Portfolio Non-Serviced Loan Combination), which is serviced under
the pooling and servicing agreement for the BMARK 2021-B24 securitization (the BMARK 2021-B24 PSA), replacing Greystone Servicing Company LLC as special servicer for such non-serviced loan combination. Midland was appointed at the direction of Eightfold Real Estate Capital, L.P., the directing certificateholder under the BMARK 2021-B24 PSA. As special servicer for the Boca Office Portfolio Non-Serviced Loan Combination, Midland will be responsible for the servicing and administration
of the Boca Office Portfolio Non-Serviced Loan Combination if it becomes specially serviced (and the servicing and administration of any related REO property) and processing and performing certain reviews of material actions with respect to the Boca Office Portfolio Non-Serviced Loan Combination when the Boca Office Portfolio Non-Serviced Loan Combination is not specially serviced. Servicing of the Boca Office Portfolio Non-Serviced Loan Combination will continue to be governed by the BMARK 2021-B24
PSA.
