BancPlus Corporation Announces Quarterly Cash Dividend

RIDGELAND, Miss. (January 24, 2025) BancPlus Corporation, the holding company for BankPlus, today announced that on January 23, 2025, its Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.50 per share of its common stock. The cash dividend represents a 6.38% increase from the previous quarter and is equivalent to an annualized dividend of $2.00 per share. The cash dividend will be paid on March 14, 2025, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on February 14, 2025.

###

About BancPlus Corporation 

BancPlus Corporation is the holding company of BankPlus. Founded in 1909, BankPlus is one of the Southeasts premier regional banks serving consumers and businesses with the latest technology through a full suite of financial services, including retail banking, commercial banking, mortgage lending and wealth management. With over $7.9 billion in total assets, BankPlus operates 84 financial centers throughout Mississippi, Alabama, Louisiana, and Florida. For more information about BankPlus, visit www.bankplus.net.
