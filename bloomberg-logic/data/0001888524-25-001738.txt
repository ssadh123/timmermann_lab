Item 6.04 Failure to Make a Required Distribution.

The
Certificate Administrator made distributions to the Certificateholders on the
January 10, 2025 Distribution Date. The Certificate Administrator was
subsequently notified by the Master Servicer on February 5, 2025 of receipt of
a curtailment payment on the State Farm Portfolio Mortgage Loan (Loan Number 2
on Annex A-1 of the prospectus supplement of the registrant relating to the
issuing entity filed on June 4, 2014 pursuant to Rule 424(b)(5)). This
resulted in an additional principal payment to the Class A-M Certificateholders
in an amount equal to $585,690.55. The additional principal payment was
made to the Class A-M Certificateholders on February 7, 2025.
