Item 8.01 Other Events.

December 31, 2024, NAV per Common Share

On January 24, 2025, ExchangeRight Income Fund Trustee, LLC (the Trustee), which serves as the sole trustee of ExchangeRight Income Fund, doing business as ExchangeRight Essential Income REIT (the Company), declared the net asset value (NAV) of the Company as of December 31, 2024 to be $698,872,000 (or $27.37 per Class I, Class A, and Class S common share as well as the common units of limited partnership interest (OP Units) in ExchangeRight Income Fund Operating Partnership, LP (the Operating Partnership). The NAV set forth above is based on the midpoint of the range of real estate values as of December 31, 2024 of the Companys owned properties as determined by an independent valuation firm, combined with the value of the Companys cash and cash equivalents, restricted cash, and other assets, and less the Companys liabilities. We calculate NAV per share in accordance with the valuation guidelines that have been approved by our Trustee. Our NAV per unit/share, which is updated as of the last calendar day of each quarter, will be posted on our website at www.exchangeright.com/the-essential-income-reit/class-i-shares/and is the same for each of our Class I, Class A, and Class S common shares and OP Units. Class ER common shares do not have a quarterly NAV per share declared given they have a stated repurchase value and are not eligible to participate in the share repurchase plan. For a description of how our NAV is calculated, see the disclosures under the caption Net Asset Value beginning on page 59 of our Form 10-K filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on April 11, 2024. Our Trustee is ultimately responsible for determining our NAV.

Private Offering Updates

The Company is currently conducting a private placement offering on a continuous basis of up to $2.165 billion of common shares of beneficial interest (the Common Shares), pursuant to which the Company is offering its Class I, Class A, Class S, and Class ER Common Shares (the Private Offering). As of the date of this report, the Company has not issued any Class S Common Shares in the Private Offering. The Company has updated the purchase price for our Class I, Class A, and Class S Common Shares offered in the Private Offering to be equal to the NAV per Common Share as of December 31, 2024, plus any applicable upfront selling commissions and expenses related to each class of Common Shares. The purchase price for the Companys Class ER Common Shares pursuant to the terms of the Private Offering is $28.97 and was not updated as of December 31, 2024. December 2024 Dividends

On December 31, 2024, the Company declared dividends in the amount of $0.1449 per share for each class of its outstanding Common Shares. The dividends for each class of Common Shares were payable to shareholders of record immediately following the close of business on December 31, 2024 and were paid in cash or reinvested in the Companys Common Shares through the Companys Dividend Reinvestment and Direct Share Purchase Plan (DRIP) on January 15, 2025.

Dividend Reinvestment and Direct Share Purchase Plan

For the December 31, 2024 declared dividends described above, holders of Common Shares and holders of Class I and Class A OP Units of the Operating Partnership have elected 10.8% of the aggregate declared dividends on Common Shares and distributions on Class I and Class A OP Units to be reinvested back into the Companys Common Shares based on elections by the individual shareholders and unitholders pursuant to the DRIP. These distributions were reinvested in the Companys Common Shares on January 15, 2025.
