Item
3.01 Notice of Delisting or Failure to Satisfy a Continued Listing Rule or Standard; Transfer of Listing

On
January 10, 2025, Lottery.com Inc. (the Company or the Registrant) received a written notice (the Notice)
from the Listing Qualifications Staff of The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC (Nasdaq) advising the Company that the Company is
no longer in compliance with Nasdaq Listing Rule 5620(a) due to its failure to hold an annual meeting of shareholders within twelve months
from the end of the Companys fiscal year 2023 (the Annual Meeting Requirement).

The
Company shall hold its 2024 Annual Meeting of its Stockholders on February 17, 2025, at 10:00 am (Central Time).
