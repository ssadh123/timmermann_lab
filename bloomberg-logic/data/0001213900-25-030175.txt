Nutriband Signs
Associate Partnership With Charlotte FC

ORLANDO, FL / ACCESSWIRE / April 4, 2025 / Nutriband Inc.
(the Company)(NASDAQ:NTRB)(NASDAQ:NTRBW), a developer of transdermal pharmaceutical products, today announced that it has
signed an Associate Partnership agreement with Charlotte FC.

The Company intends to use the Associate Partnership to build visibility
for its brands such as AI Tape which is manufactured locally in the Charlotte area.

We are very excited to partner with an organization such as
Charlotte FC as an Associate Partner. Manufacturing many of our products locally in the Charlotte region through our Pocono subsidiary
makes this relationship special.

AI Tape is manufactured in the USA at the Companys Pocono Pharmaceutical
facility in North Carolina.

The Company also intends to use the relationship to promote its platform
technology AVERSA and raise awareness for the technology which has the potential to be the Worlds first and only abuse deterrent patch
platform for managing chronic pain.

About Nutriband Inc.

We are primarily engaged in the development of a portfolio of transdermal
pharmaceutical products. Our lead product under development is an abuse deterrent fentanyl patch incorporating our AVERSA abuse
deterrence technology. AVERSA technology can be incorporated into any transdermal patch to prevent the abuse, misuse, diversion,
and accidental exposure of drugs with abuse potential.

The Companys website is www.nutriband.com.
Any material contained in or derived from the Companys websites or any other website is not part of this press release.
