Item 7.01 

Regulation FD Disclosures.

SouthState Corporation to announce quarterly earnings results on Thursday, April 24, 2025

Winter Haven, FL. April 4, 2025 SouthState Corporation (NYSE: SSB) (SouthState) announced today that it will release first quarter 2025 earnings results on Thursday, April 24, 2025, after the market closes. Upon release, investors may access a copy of SouthStates earnings results at the Companys website at www.SouthStateBank.com under Investor Relations, News, News & Market Data section. 

SouthState will host a conference call on Friday, April 25, 2025 at 9:00 a.m. (ET) to discuss its first quarter 2025 results. Investors may call in (toll free) by dialing (888) 350-3899 within the US and (646) 960-0343 for all other locations (host: Will Matthews, CFO). The numbers for international participants are listed at https://events.q4irportal.com/custom/access/2324/. The conference ID number is 4200408. Participants may also pre-register for the conference by navigating to https://events.q4inc.com/attendee/812320624. Access detail will be provided via email upon completion of registration. 

Alternatively, individuals may listen to the live webcast of the presentation by visiting the link at SouthStates website at www.SouthStateBank.com. An audio replay of the live webcast is expected to be available by the evening of Friday, April 25, 2025 through the Investor Relations section of www.SouthStateBank.com.

In accordance with General Instruction B.2 of Form 8-K, the information in this Current Report on Form 8-K shall not be deemed to be filed for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the Exchange Act), or otherwise subject to the liability of that section, and shall not be incorporated by reference into any registration statement or other document filed under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.
