Explanatory Note

The Current Report on Form 8-K (the "Original Form 8-K") filed on August 21, 2024 by Stabilis Solutions, Inc. (the "Company"), reported under Item 5.07, voting results of the Company's Annual Meeting held on August 21, 2024. This Amendment No.1 to the Original Form 8-K filed is filed solely to disclose the Company's decision regarding how frequently the Company will conduct future advisory votes on the compensation of named executive officers ("Say on Pay Votes"). No other changes have been made to the Original Form 8-K and this Amendment should be read in conjunction with the Original Form 8-K.

Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

The Board of Directors determined the Company will hold future votes to approve executive compensation every three years until the occurrence of the next advisory vote on the frequency of Say on Pay Votes or the Board of Directors resolves to modify such frequency. This frequency decision was made in light of the results of the Annual Meeting and matches the frequency recommended by the Board of Directors as disclosed in the proxy statement for the Annual Meeting and approved by its shareholders in the frequency vote.
