Item
8.01 Other Events

Background

On
January 15, 2025 at 4 PM Eastern time, the Company presented an online Investor Presentation, which was available to the public
at large. Within that video presentation, the Companys Chief Financial Strategy Officer mistakenly but inadvertently stated that
for the quarter ending November 30, 2023 the Company earned $386,000 in revenue, which produced 350% growth compared to the quarter ended
November 30, 2024.

Correction
of an Error

In
fact, the Company earned $596,000 of revenues for the quarter ended November 30, 2023, and not the $386,000 mistakenly but inadvertently
stated, which equates to a growth rate of 190% instead of the 350% mistakenly but inadvertently stated.

The
information in this Current Report on Form 8-K with respect to Item 8.01 (including the press release attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1)
is being furnished pursuant to Item 8.01 of Form 8-K and shall not be deemed to be filed for the purposes of Section 18
of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (Exchange Act), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that section,
nor shall it be deemed to be incorporated by reference in any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act.
This current report on Form 8-K (including Exhibit 99.1) will not be deemed an admission as to the materiality of any information contained
herein.
