Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

Resignation of Matthew Breslin, Chief Revenue Officer

On January 28, 2025, Matthew Breslin, the Chief Revenue Officer of Upland Software, Inc. (the Company), informed the Company that he will resign effective February 7, 2025. Mr. Breslins responsibilities will be assumed by current executives at the Company. Mr. Breslins decision to resign is not the result of any disagreement with us on any matter related to our operations, policies, or procedures.
