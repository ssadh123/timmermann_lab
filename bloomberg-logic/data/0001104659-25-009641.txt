Item 2.02. Results of Operations and Financial Condition.

On February 5, 2025,
Journey Medical Corporation (the Journey Medical) announced that it expects to report that it had $20.3 million in
cash and cash equivalents as of December 31, 2024. Additionally, Journey Medical is reaffirming its previously issued guidance for the
full year ended December 31, 2024 of:

$55 to $60 million in net product revenue;

selling, general and administrative expense in the range of $39 to $42 million; and

research and development expense in the range of $8 to $10 million.

These estimates are based
on information currently available to management. Journey Medicals actual results are not expected to vary materially from the
