Section 8 - Other Events

Item 8.01 Other Events.

On April 3, 2025, the Board of Directors
of First Real Estate Investment Trust of New Jersey, Inc. (FREIT or the Trust) declared a second quarter
dividend of $0.08 per share on the Common Stock of the Trust to holders of record of said shares at the close of business on May 30,
2025. The payment date for the dividend is June 13, 2025. The Board of Directors will continue to evaluate the dividend on a quarterly
basis and there can be no assurance that dividends will be declared for any future period. In addition, the amount of the dividend declared
on April 3, 2025 is not necessarily indicative of the amount of any dividends that may be declared in the future.
