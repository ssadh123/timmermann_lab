Item 8.01. 

Other Events.

As previously disclosed, on February 5, 2024, Veradigm Inc. (the Company) and U.S. Bank Trust Company, National Association, as successor in interest to U.S. Bank National Association, as trustee (the Trustee), entered into the first supplemental indenture (the First Supplemental Indenture) to the indenture, dated as of December 9, 2019 (the Indenture), relating to the Companys 0.875% Convertible Senior Notes due 2027 (the Notes), between the Company and the Trustee, to amend and supplement the provisions of the Indenture. In connection therewith, the Indenture was revised to, among other things, provide for biannual repurchase rights at the option of the holders of the Notes (the Noteholders) beginning on January 1, 2025 at the accreted prices set forth in the First Supplemental Indenture.

On January 2, 2025, the Company reported that no Noteholders elected to exercise such repurchase rights. Accordingly, all of the Notes remain outstanding.
