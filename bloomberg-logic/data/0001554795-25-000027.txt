Item 8.01 Other Event.

On February
5, 2025, Amaze Holdings Inc., a Delaware corporation (Pubco), filed a registration statement on Form S-4 (File No. 333-284697)
(the Registration Statement) with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the SEC), which filing can be accessed
through the SECs EDGAR website. The filing was made by Pubco, a wholly owned subsidiary of Fresh Vine Wine, Inc. (VINE).

Link to Registration Statement: Amaze Holdings Inc. Registration Statement

Pubco
will be the surviving public company following the closing of the previously announced proposed business combination among (i) Pubco,
(ii) VINE Merger Sub Inc., a Delaware corporation and wholly subsidiary of Pubco (VINE Merger Sub), (iii) Adifex Merger
Sub LLC, a Delaware limited liability company and wholly owned subsidiary of Pubco (Adifex Merger Sub), and (iv) Adifex
Holdings LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (Adifex). As a result of the proposed business combination, VINE will
become a wholly owned subsidiary of Pubco with holders of VINE equity interests receiving Pubco common stock, and Adifex will become a
wholly owned subsidiary of Pubco and each of the Adifex equity interests will be automatically converted into a pro rata portion of the
merger consideration in Pubco common stock.
