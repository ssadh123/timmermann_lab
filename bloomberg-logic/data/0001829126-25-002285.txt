Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.

On March 28, 2025, Unicoin Inc. (the Company) held a general meeting of shareholders from 4:00 p.m. to 5:30 p.m., Eastern
Time, during which a slide presentation containing unaudited and previously non-public financial data was presented to attendees of the
general meeting. Such presentation contained an unaudited consolidated balance sheet, an income statement and a cash flow statement for
the year ended December 31, 2024, as well as additional cost, revenue, expense and asset data, and other non-public information. A copy

the Company, whether made before or after the date hereof, regardless of any general incorporation language in such filing.
