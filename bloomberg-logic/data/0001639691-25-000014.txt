Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure.

As previously disclosed, LivaNova PLC (the Company) has been engaged in a civil action whereby the Court of Appeal of Milan previously declared the Company (formed through a merger with Sorin) jointly and severally liable with SNIA (a former parent of Sorin) for environmental liabilities incurred by SNIAs other subsidiaries in the amount of 454 million ($493 million as of March 14, 2025). On February 26, 2025, the Italian Supreme Court held a public hearing in connection with the SNIA litigation, and on March 14, 2025, the Italian Supreme Court issued its decision in response to all of the appeals of the Company and counter-appeals submitted by the Ministry of Ecological Transition, the Ministry of Economy and Finance, and the Prime Ministers Office. 

Pursuant to the decision, the Italian Supreme Court determined that the Company can be held jointly and severally liable for the established liabilities of SNIA at the time of demerger as well as the environmental liabilities of the demerged company that materialized after the demerger which are derived from actions performed prior to the demerger; however, the Italian Supreme Court also ruled that the Company should not be held responsible for certain payments previously approved by the Court of Appeal of Milan in the amount of 157 million ($171 million as of March 14, 2025). The case is now referred back to the Court of Appeal of Milan to implement the decisions respecting costs and damages in accordance with the judgment of the Italian Supreme Court.

As a result of the decision by the Italian Supreme Court, the Company expects to record a liability of approximately 333 million (approximately $363 million as of March 14, 2025) for the three months ended March 31, 2025 as the best estimate of the liability inclusive of estimated costs, fees, interest, and taxes. Given its cash and cash equivalents of $429 million and restricted cash of $295 million as of December 31, 2024, the Company has determined that it has sufficient resources to cover the liability and will provide further details on its upcoming first-quarter 2025 earnings call.
