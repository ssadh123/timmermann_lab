About Atlantic Union Bankshares Corporation
Headquartered in Richmond, Virginia, Atlantic Union Bankshares Corporation (NYSE:
AUB) is the holding company for Atlantic Union Bank. Atlantic Union Bank had 129
branches located throughout Virginia and in portions of Maryland and North Carolina as
of December 31, 2024. Certain non-bank financial services affiliates of Atlantic Union
Bank include: Atlantic Union Equipment Finance, Inc., which provides equipment
financing; Atlantic Union Financial Consultants, LLC, which provides brokerage services;
and Union Insurance Group, LLC, which offers various lines of insurance products.
