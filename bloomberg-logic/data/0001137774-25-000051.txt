Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure.

Prudential Financial, Inc. (the Company) is furnishing this Current Report to disclose (1) certain preliminary financial information regarding the quarter ended March 31, 2025, prior to the availability of the Companys quarterly earnings release and quarterly financial supplement for the period, scheduled for release on April 30, 2025; (2) the resegmentation of its International Businesses segment; and (3) the expected results from an update to the Companys internal expense allocations that became effective January 1, 2025. 

Assets Under Management of the PGIM Segment

As of March 31, 2025, assets under management of the PGIM segment were $1.39 trillion.

Other Related Revenues, Net of Related Expenses, of the PGIM Segment

For the quarter ended March 31, 2025, PGIMs other related revenues, net of related expenses, were approximately $20 million on an adjusted operating income basis(1). Other related revenues include incentive fees, transaction fees, and revenues from seed and co-investments and mortgage originations.

Alternative Investment Income of the General Account

For the quarter ended March 31, 2025, the alternative investment income of the Companys General Account, excluding the Closed Block Division and Funds Withheld portfolios, (the GA Portfolio) is estimated to be approximately $85 - $105 million below the Companys near-term expectations. Alternative investment income includes the adjusted operating income of the GA Portfolios interests in private equity, hedge fund and real estate-related investments, as well as real estate investments held through direct ownership, and excludes the results from those interests held by Divested Businesses and unaffiliated investors that have been consolidated on the Companys balance sheet.

The preliminary financial information presented above for the quarter ended March 31, 2025, is estimated and unaudited and has been prepared in good faith on a basis consistent with prior periods based on information available to management as of the date hereof; however, we have not completed our financial closing procedures for the quarter ended March 31, 2025, and our actual results could be materially different from these preliminary financial results.

International Businesses Resegmentation

Effective in the first quarter of 2025, consistent with changes to the Companys internal management structure, the International Businesses division will be reflected as a single operating and reportable segment, which is consistent with how the Companys chief executive officer will now assess its performance and allocate resources. Prior to the first quarter of 2025, the International Businesses division consisted of the Life Planner and Gibraltar Life and Other operating segments, each of which was a reportable segment under U.S. GAAP. The change will be applied retrospectively and does not have an impact on any previously issued financial results.

Expense Allocation Update

Effective January 1, 2025, following an annual review of its internal expense allocations, the Company implemented an allocation update that will impact segment results; however, there will be no impact to the Companys consolidated results. The table below shows the approximate expected resulting impacts to segment adjusted operating income for the year ending December 31, 2025. As a result of this update, the full-year 2025 loss for the Companys Corporate & Other operations is now expected to be approximately $1.7 billion.
