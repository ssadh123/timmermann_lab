Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

Effective February 16, 2025, Steven Sorhus tendered his resignation as Financial Controller and member of the Management Committee of Verde Resources, Inc (the Company).

There have been no disagreements between the Company and Mr. Sorhus, to the knowledge of any executive officer, regarding the Company's operations, policies, or practices. Mr. Sorhus was provided a copy of this disclosure no later than the date of this Form 8-K filing and was given the opportunity to submit a letter stating whether he agrees with the statements made herein. If received, Mr. Sorhus letter will be filed as an exhibit to this Form 8-K via amendment within three business days of receipt.

Effective February 16, 2025, the Board of Directors has appointed Duka Donaghy as Director of Finance, replacing Steven Sorhus in his former role as Financial Controller and as a member of the Management Committee.
