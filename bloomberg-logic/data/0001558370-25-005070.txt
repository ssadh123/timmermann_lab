Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

On April 17, 2025, Innovative Solutions and Support, Inc. (the Company) held its annual meeting of shareholders (the Annual Meeting). 

Present at the Annual Meeting in person or by proxy were holders of 11,445,496 shares of common stock of the Company, representing 65.2% of the shares of common stock of the Company issued and outstanding and entitled to vote as of the close of business on February 20, 2025, the record date for the Annual Meeting, and constituting a quorum for the transaction of business.
