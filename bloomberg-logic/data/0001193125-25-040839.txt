Item 3.02 

Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities. 

On February 1, 2025, Blackstone Infrastructure Strategies L.P. (the Fund) sold unregistered limited partnership units (the Units) for aggregate consideration of approximately $153.6 million.The following table details the Units sold by the Fund: 

Number of Units Sold(1) Consideration 

Class I 2,656,264 $ 67,430,863 

Class S 2,923,158 $ 74,153,500 

Class D 472,808 $ 12,000,000 

(1) 

The number of Units sold by the Fund was finalized on February 27, 2025, following the calculation of the Funds transactional net asset value (Transactional NAV) as of January 31, 2025. See Item 7.01 below for more information on the Funds Transactional NAV. 

The offer and sale of the Units were made as part of the Funds continuous private offering and were exempt from the registration provisions of the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, pursuant to Section 4(a)(2) and Regulation D thereunder. Units were sold to third-party investors, including through Blackstone Infrastructure Strategies (TE) L.P., a Delaware limited partnership for certain investors with particular tax characteristics, such as tax-exempt investors and non-U.S. investors.

Transactional NAV as

of January 31, 2025 

Class I $ 25.39

As of January 31, 2025, BXINFRAs aggregate Transactional NAV was approximately $1.1 billion. 

The Fund calculates Transactional NAV for purposes of establishing the price at which transactions in the Funds Units are made. A description of the Funds valuation process was included under Item 9. Market Price of and Dividends on the Registrants Common Equity and Related Unitholder MattersCalculation of Net Asset Value of the Funds Post Effective Amendment No. 1 to its Registration Statement on Form 10 filed on November 13, 2024. Transactional NAV is based on the month-end values of the Funds investments and other assets and the deduction of any liabilities, including certain fees and expenses, in all cases as determined in accordance with the Funds valuation policy that has been approved by the Funds board of directors. Organization and offering expenses advanced on the Funds behalf by the Funds investment manager are recognized as a reduction to Transactional NAV ratably over 60 months beginning on January 1, 2026, and unitholder servicing fees, as applicable, are recognized as a reduction to Transactional NAV on a monthly basis as such fees are accrued. Certain contingent tax liabilities may not be recognized as a reduction to Transactional NAV if the Funds general partner reasonably expects such liabilities will not be recognized upon divestment of the underlying investment. Transactional NAV per Unit may differ from the Funds net asset value as determined in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America.
