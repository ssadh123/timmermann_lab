Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders

On April 15, 2025, Prosperity Bancshares, Inc. (the Company) held its Annual Meeting of Shareholders (the Annual Meeting). Holders of 86,415,224 shares of the Companys outstanding common stock (Common Stock), representing 90.71% of the shares entitled to vote as of the record date for the Annual Meeting, participated in person or by proxy.

The matters voted on at the Annual Meeting were:

1.

Election of Directors. 

Ileana Blanco, Leah Henderson, Ned S. Holmes, Jack Lord and David Zalman were elected as Class III directors to serve on the Board of Directors of the Company (the Board) until the Companys 2028 Annual Meeting of Shareholders, and each until their successors are duly elected and qualified, or until their earlier resignation or removal.
