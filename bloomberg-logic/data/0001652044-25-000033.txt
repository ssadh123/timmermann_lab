Item 8.01. Other Events.

On April 17, 2025, Judge Leonie M. Brinkema of U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Virginia issued a mixed decision in the U.S. Department of Justices (DOJ) case against Google LLC ("Google"), a subsidiary of Alphabet Inc., ruling that the DOJ failed to show that Googles advertiser tools or acquisitions of DoubleClick and AdMeld were anticompetitive, but that Googles publisher tools violated antitrust laws by excluding rivals. Google plans to appeal the adverse portion of this decision.
