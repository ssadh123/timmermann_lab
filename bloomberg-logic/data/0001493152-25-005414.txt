Certificate
of Amendment of Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation

On
January 27, 2025, at the Special Meeting of Common Stock Shareholders, CareCloud, Inc.s (the Company) shareholders
approved an amendment to the Companys amended and restated certificate of incorporation (the Certificate of Incorporation)
to amend Section 4.1 of the Certificate of Incorporation to increase the number of authorized shares of the Companys common stock
that may be issued from thirty-five million shares to eighty-five million shares, as described in the Companys Proxy Statement
filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on December 5, 2024. The Certificate of Incorporation was filed with the Secretary
of State of the State of Delaware on February 5, 2025 and became effective on such date.
