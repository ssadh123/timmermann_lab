ITEM
5.03 Amendments to Articles of Incorporation or Bylaws.

As
reported in the Current Report on Form 8-K filed by Aceztech Corporation (the Company) on January 24, 2025, the Company
filed a Certificate of Amendment and Restated Certificate of Incorporation (the Certificate of Amendment) amending its
Articles of Incorporation with the Nevada Secretary of State on February 3, 2025 to make effective for the name change of the Company
from Aceztech Corporation to Huineng Technology Corporation (the Name Change).

On
January 21, 2025, the Company filed an Issuer Company-Related Action Notification Form with Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA)
to request effectiveness of the name change and a new stock symbol (the Symbol Change).

On
February 14, 2025, FINRA announced that the Name Change and Symbol Change would be made effective in the marketplace as of market open
on February 18, 2025.

Additionally,
FINRA approved the Companys request to change its stock symbol from ACZT to HNIT (the Symbol
Change).

No
action is required from current shareholders in relation to the announcement of the Name Change and Symbol Change. The Companys
CUSIP is remained.

The
foregoing description is qualified in its entirety by the full text of the Certificate of Amendment, which is filed as Exhibit 3.1 to
this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.
