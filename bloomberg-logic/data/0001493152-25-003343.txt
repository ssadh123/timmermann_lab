Item
8.01. Other Events.

Overpayment
in the Redemption of the Second Extension Redeeming Stockholders and Correction for the Overpayment Amount

On
February 14, 2024, FutureTech II Acquisition Corp. (the Company) held a special meeting of stockholders (the Second
Extension Meeting). In connection with the Second Extension Meeting, as set forth in the Companys Report on Form 8-K
filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the SEC) on February 14, 2024, the holders of 3,236,915
shares of the Companys Class A common stock (the Public Shares) properly exercised their right to
redeem (the Second Extension Redeeming Stockholders) such
shares for a pro rata portion of the funds in the Companys trust account (Trust Account).

On
February 22, 2024, a redemption payment was made by Continental Stock Transfer & Trust Company (CST), as trustee
of the Trust Account, to the Second Extension Redeeming Stockholders at a rate of $11.20881763 per share (the Second Redemption
Payment). It was later determined that the Company did not withdraw all of the interest from the Trust Account that it was
allowed to withdraw to cover income and franchise taxes and, therefore, the Second Redemption Payment should have been $11.09703114 per
share. This meant that the Second Extension Redeeming Stockholders were overpaid in the amount of $0.11178649 per share (the Second
Extension Overpayment Amount).

The
Second Extension Redeeming Stockholders are in the process of being notified of this situation and are being instructed to return the
Second Extension Overpayment Amount to CST. Anyone with questions about the contents of this report, can reach out to CST at spacredemptions@continentalstock.com.
