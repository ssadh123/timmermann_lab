Item 8.01. Other Events.

Mawson Infrastructure Group Inc. (NASDAQ: MIGI) ("Mawson"
or "the Company"), a publicly-traded technology company focused on digital infrastructure platforms for artificial intelligence
(AI), high-performance computing (HPC), and digital assets markets, announced today that it will participate in the Roth Capital Annual
Conference, and the NVIDIA GTC AI Conference in March, 2025.

Mawson will also participate in the 37th Annual Roth Capital Conference
taking place March 16-18, 2025, in Dana Point, California. The Annual Roth Conference event is one of the premier U.S. investor conferences
bringing together institutional investors, sector analysts, industry leaders, and growth companies.

Mawson will also join the NVIDIA GTC AI Conference taking place March
17-21, 2025 in San Jose, California. NVIDIAs GPU Technology Conference is a global artificial intelligence (AI) conference focused
on applications of artificial intelligence, high-performance computing, and deep learning.

For more information about Mawson Infrastructure Group or to see the
full updated Company Presentation, visit the Companys website at www.mawsoninc.com/company-presentations

Companys Media Interviews and Videos are available at www.mawsoninc.com/video-interviews
and Articles and News related to the Company are available at www.mawsoninc.com/articles
