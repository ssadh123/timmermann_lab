Item 8.01 OTHER EVENTS.

Portfolio Update

As of February 28, 2025, TriLinc Global Impact Fund, LLCs (TGIF, the Company, we, our, or us) portfolio investments in borrowers for business expansions and socioeconomic developments were fair valued at approximately $291 million. The weighted average loan size of the portfolio is approximately $10 million, with a weighted average duration of 0.4 years. As of February 28, 2025, TGIF has funded approximately $1.210 billion in aggregate investments to 103 borrower companies globally, (including $104.7 million in temporary investments) supporting 42,855 permanent employees. Of the aggregate investment amount, TGIF has received approximately $895.3 million in full aggregate transaction repayments (74% of total invested) from existing and exited trade finance, term loan, and temporary investment facilities.
