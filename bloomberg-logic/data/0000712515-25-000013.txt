Item 8.01 Other Events.

On February 5, 2025, Electronic Arts Inc. ("EA" or the "Company"), entered into an accelerated share repurchase agreement (the ASR Agreement) with Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC (Goldman Sachs). Under the ASR Agreement, the Company will purchase an aggregate of $1.0 billion (the Repurchase Price) of the Companys common stock, par value $0.01 per share (the Common Stock), as part of its previously announced $5 billion stock repurchase program.

Under the terms of the ASR Agreement, on February 6, 2025, the Company will pay the Repurchase Price and receive Common Stock with an aggregate value of 80% of the Repurchase Price based on the closing price of the Common Stock on February 5, 2025. The total number of shares that the Company will ultimately repurchase under the ASR Agreement will be determined based on the volume-weighted average price of the Common Stock during the term of the ASR Agreement, less a discount and subject to adjustments pursuant to the terms and conditions of the ASR Agreement. Final settlement of the ASR Agreement is expected to occur no later than the end of EAs first fiscal quarter of fiscal year 2026.
