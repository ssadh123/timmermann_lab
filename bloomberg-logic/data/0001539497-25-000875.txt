Section 8 Other Events

Item 8.01 Other Events.

Effective as of March 10, 2025, Midland Loan Services, a Division of PNC Bank, National Association (Midland), a national banking association, will act as special servicer for the 33 West State Street and 50 East State Street mortgage loan and each related pari passu and/or subordinate promissory note (collectively, the 33 West State Street and 50 East State Street Non-Serviced
Loan Combination), which is serviced under the pooling and servicing agreement for the BMO 2024-5C3 Mortgage Trust securitization (the BMO 2024-5C3 Mortgage Trust PSA), replacing Greystone Servicing Company LLC as special servicer for such non-serviced loan combination. Midland was appointed at the direction of Eightfold Real Estate Capital, L.P., the directing certificateholder under the BMO 2024-5C3 Mortgage Trust PSA. As special servicer for the 33 West State Street
and 50 East State Street Non-Serviced Loan Combination, Midland will be responsible for the servicing and administration of the 33 West State Street and 50 East State Street Non-Serviced Loan Combination if it becomes specially serviced (and the servicing and administration of any related REO property) and processing and performing certain reviews of material actions with respect to the 33 West State Street and 50 East State Street Non-Serviced Loan Combination when the 33 West State Street and
50 East State Street Non-Serviced Loan Combination is not specially serviced. Servicing of the 33 West State Street and 50 East State Street Non-Serviced Loan Combination will continue to be governed by the BMO 2024-5C3 Mortgage Trust PSA.
