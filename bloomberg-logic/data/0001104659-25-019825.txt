ITEM 7.01. REGULATION FD DISCLOSURE.

On March 3, 2025, Stewart
Information Services Corporation (the Company) issued a press release announcing that its Board of Directors has declared
a cash dividend of $0.50 per share of the Companys common stock for the first quarter 2025, payable March 31, 2025, to common
stockholders of record on March 17, 2025. A copy of the press release is furnished herewith as Exhibit 99.1.

The information in this Item
7.01 and Exhibit 99.1 attached hereto is not deemed to be filed for the purposes of Section 18 of the Securities
Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (Exchange Act), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that section, nor shall such
information and exhibit be deemed incorporated by reference in any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange
Act, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.
