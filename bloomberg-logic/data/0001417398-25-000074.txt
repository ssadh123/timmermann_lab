Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

On February 18, 2025, Hillenbrand, Inc. (the Company) held its Annual Meeting of shareholders (the Annual Meeting). At the Annual Meeting, the Companys shareholders voted upon the following proposals:

(1) the election of four (4) members of the Companys Board of Directors;

(2) the approval, by a non-binding advisory vote, of the compensation paid by the Company to its Named Executive Officers (Say on Pay Vote); and

(3) the ratification of the appointment of Ernst & Young LLP as the Companys independent registered public accounting firm for fiscal year 2025.
