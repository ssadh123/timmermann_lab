ITEM 8.01. 

OTHER EVENTS. 

On February 11, 2025, The Bank of New York Mellon Corporation issued $1,250,000,000 aggregate principal amount of its 4.942% Fixed Rate / Floating Rate Callable Senior Medium-Term Notes Series J due 2031 (the Notes). The Notes were registered under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, pursuant to a registration statement on Form S-3 (File No. 333-282710) (as amended, the Registration Statement). In connection with this issuance, Exhibits 5.1 and 23.1 are filed as part of this Current Report on Form 8-K and are incorporated by reference into the Registration Statement.
