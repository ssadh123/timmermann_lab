Knowles Reports Q1 2025 Financial Results and Provides Outlook for Q2 2025

First Quarter Revenues from Continuing Operations of $132M, at High-End of the Guided Range

Net Cash from Operations Exceeded the High-End of the Guided Range

Q2 Revenues are Expected to be up 4% Year over Year

ITASCA, Ill., April 24, 2025 - Knowles Corporation (NYSE: KN), a leading manufacturer of specialty electronic components for innovative technologies, including capacitors, radio frequency ("RF") filters, advanced medtech microphones, and balanced armature speakers, today announced results for the quarter ended March 31, 2025.

I am pleased we completed the first quarter of 2025 with revenues at the high end of our guided range, cash provided by operating activities above our guided range, and non-GAAP diluted EPS from continuing operations at the mid-point of our guided range, commented Jeffrey Niew, President, and CEO of Knowles. 

Our revenue outlook for the second quarter represents sequential and year over year growth of 6% and 4%, respectively. 

Our Precision Devices segment continues to experience increased order activity and backlog in most end markets while the MedTech and Specialty Audio business continues to perform at expectations. Mr. Niew continued. While there is macro level uncertainty due to tariffs, we are not seeing any slowdown in demand. Based on this, I am increasingly confident that we will see year over year growth for 2025 across our businesses.

The company repurchased $5 million of shares and reduced debt by $15 million in Q1 and we expect another year of strong cash generation in 2025. We will provide details of our plans for 2025 and beyond at our upcoming Investor Day which will take place on May 13th.

About Knowles 

Knowles is a leading manufacturer of specialty electronic components. We design parts that perform unique, critical functions for innovative technologies. Through extreme reliability, custom engineering, and scalable manufacturing, we enable businesses to succeed in the most demanding applications across medtech, defense, and industrial markets.

Our high-performance capacitors, RF and microwave filters, advanced medtech microphones, balanced armature speakers, and miniaturization products enable and enhance the performance of technologies with the power to change, improve, and save lives. Founded in 1946 and headquartered in Itasca, Illinois, Knowles has grown into a global organization with employees spanning 11 countries.

For more information, please visit knowles.com.
