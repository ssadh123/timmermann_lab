Item 8.01 Other Events. 

On March 21, 2025, Enbridge Inc. (the Company) began mailing its Notice of 2025 Annual Meeting and Notice of availability of meeting materials (the Notice) to shareholders of the Company. Copies of the Notice mailed to registered shareholders and to beneficial shareholders of the Company are attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and Exhibit 99.2, respectively. Information and instructions on how to attend the virtual 2025 Annual Meeting of Shareholders are contained in the Management Information Circular.
