ABOUT CION INVESTMENT CORPORATION

CION Investment Corporation
is a leading publicly listed business development company that had approximately $1.9 billion in total assets as of December 31, 2024.
CION seeks to generate current income and, to a lesser extent, capital appreciation for investors by focusing primarily on senior secured
loans to U.S. middle-market companies. CION is advised by CION Investment Management, LLC, a registered investment adviser and an affiliate
of CION. For more information, please visit www.cionbdc.com.
