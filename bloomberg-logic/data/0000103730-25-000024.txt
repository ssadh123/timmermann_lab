conversion price for each issuance of the Company's convertible senior notes, specifically, the 2.25% Convertible Senior Notes due 2025 (the "2025 Notes"). As required by the indentures governing the terms of each issuance of convertible senior
notes, the Company has delivered a notice and officers' certificate to the trustees regarding these adjustments.
