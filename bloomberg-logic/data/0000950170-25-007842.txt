Item 5.02. Departure of Directors or Principal Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Principal Officers.

On January 21, 2025, the Board of Directors of Ashland Inc. (Ashland or the Company) appointed J. Kevin Willis, the Companys Senior Vice President, Chief Financial Officer and Principal Financial Officer, to also serve as the Companys Principal Accounting Officer, effective January 21, 2025.
