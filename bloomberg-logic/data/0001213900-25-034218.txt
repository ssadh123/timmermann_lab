7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure.

On April 18, 2025, Aditxt, Inc. (the Company)
hosted a weekly update featuring Amro Albanna, the Chief Executive Officer of the Company, and Steve Varvel, the Director of Clinical
Trials of the Companys subsidiary, Pearsanta, Inc. (Pearsanta) which highlighted Pearsantas recent
IRB approval to initiate clinical studies of its blood-based diagnostic test for endometriosis.. A copy of the transcript of the weekly
update is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.

out into circulation.
