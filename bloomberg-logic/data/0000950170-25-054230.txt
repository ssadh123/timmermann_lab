Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

Amendment to Revolving Credit Agreement

PureCycle Technologies, Inc. (the Company) is party to that certain Credit Agreement (as amended, the Revolving Credit Agreement) dated as of March 15, 2023, with PureCycle Technologies Holdings Corp., PureCycle Technologies, LLC, and PureCycle Augusta, LLC (collectively, the Guarantors), Sylebra Capital Partners Master Fund, LTD, Sylebra Capital Parc Master Fund, and Sylebra Capital Menlo Master Fund (collectively, the Lenders), and Madison Pacific Trust Limited (the Administrative Agent and Security Agent), providing the Company with a revolving credit facility pursuant to which the Company can borrow up to $200 million (the Revolving Credit Facility). On April 11, 2025, the Company, the Guarantors, the Administrative Agent, the Security Agent and the Lenders executed an Eighth Amendment to the Credit Agreement (Eighth Amendment to Credit Agreement), which amends the Revolving Credit Agreement to extend the maturity date of the Revolving Credit Facility from March 31, 2026 to September 30, 2026. The Lenders and their affiliates are greater than 5% beneficial owners of the Company.

The foregoing summary of the Eighth Amendment to Credit Agreement is subject to, and qualified in its entirety by, the text of the Eighth Amendment to Credit Agreement, which is filed as Exhibit 10.1 hereto and incorporated herein by reference.

Side Letter

On April 11, 2025, the Company entered into a Side Letter (the Side Letter) with Sylebra Capital Management (Sylebra Capital), pursuant to which the Company agreed to forbear the exercise of its redemption rights with respect to the Series A Warrants, distributed September 17, 2022 (the Series A Warrants), that are held by investment entities affiliated with Sylebra Capital, until the earlier of (i) the one year anniversary of the date the Companys redemption rights are triggered and (ii) the termination date of the Series A Warrants.
