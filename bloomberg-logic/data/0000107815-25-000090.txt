EXPLANATORY NOTE

On October 18, 2024, WEC Energy Group, Inc. (the Company) filed a Current Report on Form 8-K (the Original 8-K), which announced the appointment of Warner Baxter and John Lange to the Companys Board of Directors (the Board), effective January 1, 2025. At the time of the Original 8-K, the Board had not made a determination regarding any committee assignment for Mr. Baxter or Mr. Lange. This Amendment No. 1 on Form 8-K/A to the Original 8-K is being filed to disclose the committee assignments for Mr. Baxter and Mr. Lange. Except for the foregoing, this Amendment No. 1 does not amend any other disclosure reported in the Original 8-K.
