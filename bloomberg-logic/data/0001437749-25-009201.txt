Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders. 

Emerson Radio Corp. (the Company) held its annual meeting of stockholders for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2024 (the Annual Meeting) on March 20, 2025, at 9:30 a.m., Eastern Daylight Time. At the Annual Meeting, 18,309,601 shares of the Companys common stock were represented either in person or by proxy, which is equal to approximately 87% of the Companys outstanding common stock as of the February 7, 2025, record date.

The following proposals, each of which is described in detail in the Companys definitive proxy statement filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on February 18, 2025, were before the meeting, and they received the following votes:

Proposal 1: Election of Directors All director nominees were elected to hold office until the next annual meeting of stockholders or until their successor is elected and qualified.
