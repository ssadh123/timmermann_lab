Dean
W. Rivest, CEO, announced that net sales of Omega Flex, Inc. (the Company or Omega Flex) for 2024 and
2023 were $101,681,000 and $111,465,000, respectively, decreasing $9,784,000 or 8.8%. Net Sales for the fourth quarter of 2024 were 4.2%
lower than during the same period in 2023. The decrease in net sales was mainly due to lower sales unit volumes as the overall market
continued to be suppressed because of, among other factors, a decline in housing starts.

The
Companys net income for 2024 was $18,014,000, compared to $20,763,000 during 2023, decreasing $2,749,000 or 13.2%. Net income
for the fourth quarter of 2024 was 4.2% below the same period in 2023. The decrease in net income for 2024 is mainly due to the decrease
in net sales, higher salary related expenses and travel.
