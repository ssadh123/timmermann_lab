Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

The First Bancorp (Nasdaq: FNLC) announced that Christopher J. Austin has been appointed by the Board of Directors to be Clerk of the Company. 

Mr. Austin succeeds Susan A. Norton who retired from the Company on February 14, 2025. Mr. Austin joined First National Bank, the Companys wholly owned subsidiary, in 2024 and serves as its Executive Vice President, Chief Legal Counsel.
